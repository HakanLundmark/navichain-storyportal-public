# THE JOB NO ONE COULD DISPUTE
### A navichain story — Yvonne, Daniel and the proof that was in his pocket

**Persona:** Yvonne Kron, 44, coordinator/dispatcher, Kron Elteknik AB (electrical installation, heat-pump service and property services, 9 field technicians)
**Co-protagonist:** Daniel Ohls, 31, field technician at Kron Elteknik
**Supporting character:** Grels Ambjörnsson, property owner and repeat customer
**New standalone persona thread:** Kron Elteknik AB is a new, standalone thread in the series — not connected to Björklunda Åkeri or Fors Maskinuthyrning. The company represents the construction/installation/field-service segment, where an assignment is often a combination of on-site work and material delivery.
**Theme:** Field-service operations — from loose paper notes and verbal jobs to traceable bookings, mobile order handling and indisputable proof of completed work
**Navichain's role in the story:** The booking that carries the whole assignment (customer, address, time, requirements, documents) and the mobile app that captures photo, signature, time and location automatically when the job is complete — without paper, without phone calls back and forth, without an IT department
**Format:** 12 panels + cover concept, Swedish dialogue/captions in the original, warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin style (ligne claire), parchment/cream base palette with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold, hand-drawn captions, subtle print grain and patina — the story takes place in a modern context (mobile phones, booking systems) but is drawn throughout in the series' classic visual language
**Colour dramaturgy:** 🔴 Red (problem, panels 1–6) → 🟠 Orange (turning point, panel 7) → 🟢 Petroleum green (solution, panels 8–12)

**Logo rule (mandatory):** navichain's official logo is completely absent from panels 1–6. It is introduced discreetly from panel 7 onwards — as an environmental detail (the app header on a mobile screen, a coffee mug, the service van decal, a corner stamp on a document), never centred or advertising-like, max. approx. 5% of the panel area. The illustrator must use the uploaded source file for navichain's official logo — never a redrawn, stylised or self-created interpretation.

## Product terminology and grounding

This story is grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-CAPABILITY-INDEX.md, NAVICHAIN-TERMINOLOGY.md, Market Truth MASTER):

- **Booking:** The transport order/assignment unit that gathers customer, locations, dates, goods/assignment, requirements, charges/costs, documents and tracking in one place — instead of verbal handover and loose paper notes. Source: Terminology/Core entity map.
- **Mobile app (`navichain 365`):** Gives field technicians/drivers mobile assignment execution — assignment, start, partial registration, photo, signature, comment and automatic GPS/time. Source: Apps & Portals — Mobile App.
- **Mobile proof-of-delivery (proof concept):** The driver/technician records photos, signature and comments when the work is performed; GPS and timestamp are added automatically. This is the capability used in the story to document the field-service job. Source: Capability Index #128 — "Mobile proof photos/signature plus automatic GPS/time".
- **Push notifications on assignment:** Technicians are notified when a new assignment is allocated or changed directly in the app. Source: Product Truth — Automations/Mobile App.
- **B09 segment (Construction, installation and service with goods + service):** Market Truth MASTER identifies this as a distinct customer segment where the assignment often combines work on site and material, and where a booking that can carry services, materials, tasks and proof creates greater value than pure freight handling — summarised in the market message "The transport is complete only when the job is complete."

**Explicit limitation (what is NOT claimed):** The story does not claim that Navichain contains a separate "field-service module", that signatures are eIDAS-qualified, or that the technician's identity is legally verified through the signature. What is depicted is documented: the booking as a consolidated information carrier and the mobile app's recording of photo/signature/automatic GPS-time when the work is performed.

## Continuity note

This is the first story in a new, standalone persona thread: **Kron Elteknik AB**, within the construction/installation/field-service segment (B09 in Market Truth). The thread is deliberately separate from Björklunda Åkeri (haulage/transport) and Fors Maskinuthyrning (rental) to give the field-service angle a clear voice of its own — where the assignment is completed work rather than a transported shipment. Yvonne Kron and Daniel Ohls are intended to be able to return in future episodes within the same thread (e.g. material handling in the service van, or real-time day planning for several technicians).
