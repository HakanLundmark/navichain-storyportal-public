## Original user brief

New persona in the series: Anders Vikström, 43, traffic/operations manager at **Vikström Åkeri AB** — a family-owned, medium-sized haulage company with 18 vans/trucks carrying out distribution in Sweden and neighbouring EU countries. The haulier represents the typical smaller/medium-sized European transport operator: too small for its own IT department, too large to get by on gut feeling and a stack of paper.

Product angle: Navichain route optimisation — truck-adapted route planning with HERE Maps (weight, height and ADR restrictions), built-in EU 561/2006 driving-time warnings in the planning view, multi-stop optimisation and automatic real-time rerouting during traffic disruptions.

### General direction notes

- Ligne claire, warm 1950s style in the Hergé/Tintin tradition. Clean, evenly weighted contour lines, flat colour fields, minimal halftone shading.
- Base: parchment/cream, with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold.
- Hand-lettered captions, not digital sans serif.
- Lightly grainy offset-print feel with patina — subtle raster grain, paper texture and soft edge wear.
- Colour dramaturgy: red (panels 1–4, crisis) → mustard/orange (panels 5–6, transition) → petroleum green (panels 7–12, solution).
- Prefer physical props to screen mock-ups where possible; where screens are needed — modern tablets/laptops/phones drawn in the same warm ligne-claire line, not photorealistic UI.
- No IT support appears in any panel. Anders logs in, enters the details and solves everything himself — Navichain is self-explanatory.
- Navichain's official logo must be used as the actual brand logo. The logo is completely absent from panels 1–6 and introduced discreetly from panel 7 onwards as an environmental detail.
- Anders should read as genuinely warm and relieved in the resolution panels.
