# A Navichain story: "Hunting the Ghost Package – How Patrik found control at the loading bay"

**Format:** 12-panel comic script for illustrator **Visual style:** Warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin *ligne claire* — even-weight black outlines, flat/even colour fields (minimal halftone shading, no heavy cross-hatching). Parchment/cream base surface with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold. Captions hand-lettered, never digital sans-serif. Physical objects (consignment notes, barcode labels, clipboards, stamps, signs) take priority over screenshots/UI mock-ups. Surface texture: lightly grainy offset-print feel with patina — like a well-thumbed 1950s magazine: subtle paper grain, soft edge wear, muted/slightly faded colour tones. The characters are warm, expressive and genuinely happy in the solution panels — not merely neutral or relieved.

**Protagonist:** Patrik, operations manager at Svealand Transport AB **Supporting character:** Daniel, driver (drives route 3) **Assignment in the story:** Svealand Transport AB carries freight for the customer Nilssons Fjärr & Specialtransporter, with delivery to Svealand Fabrik (receiver P. Andersson) **Context:** The story takes place in a modern everyday setting — Patrik and Daniel work with navichain on their computers and mobile phones instead of paper lists and manual routines. The visual language is retro, but the tools and working methods are completely modern. **Colour dramaturgy:** 🔴 Red (crisis/problem, panels 1–4) → 🟠 Mustard yellow/orange (turning point/insight, panels 5–6) → 🟢 Petroleum green (solution confirmed, panels 7–12) **navichain logo:** Completely absent in panels 1–6. Introduced discreetly from panel 7 onwards, only as environmental props (shoulder bag, clipboard/receipt form, document header). Never exceeds about 5% of the panel area, never centred or advertising-like. The navichain logo means navichain's actual official trademark logo — not a generic "N" symbol. The illustrator must use the official logo file (provided by Håkan) at each specified placement.

### Illustrator notes

- Consistent contrast: panels 1–4 show stress, scattered papers and manual searching. From panel 6 onwards this is replaced by calm, precise movements — Patrik and Daniel handle everything themselves, directly through their own mobile phones and computers. No IT department or support function appears or is needed anywhere in the story — navichain is self-explanatory.
- The navichain logo means navichain's actual official logo (file exists in the project's source material) — never an invented "N" symbol or placeholder. Absent in panels 1–6, discreetly introduced from panel 7: shoulder bag, receipt form, coffee mug.
- Physical props — barcode labels, clipboard, archive folder, handheld scanner — throughout instead of screen interfaces, in line with the comic's ligne claire aesthetic.
- Keep the same warm 1950s palette, grainy offset feel and colour dramaturgy (red → orange → green) as the series' other episodes.
