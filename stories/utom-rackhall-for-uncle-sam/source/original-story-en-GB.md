# "Out of Reach of Uncle Sam"

### A Navichain story

**Source:** https://demosite.navichain.se/plattformen/dataintegritet/  
**Part of:** the same visual world and continuity as the other Navichain stories (Johan, Elin, Maria, Patrik, Kicki, Robban, Conny Sjövall, Anders Vikström, Erik Wallin, Petra Lindahl and others)  
**Protagonist:** Sofia Bergqvist, finance and sustainability manager at the fictional **Bohuslänska Fjärrtransport AB**  
**Supporting character:** Robban Ek — the series' recurring continuity character, here in the role of colleague/operations manager who points Sofia towards Navichain  
**Symbolic antagonist:** "Uncle Sam" — a drawn, allegorical figure (top hat, stars-and-stripes jacket, magnifying glass/listening device) representing American surveillance legislation. Never a literal scene partner — inserted as a shadow/silhouette over cloud and server motifs during the problem phase.  
**Theme:** "European data centre" does not mean European law. The US CLOUD Act and FISA 702 give US authorities access to data controlled by US companies — regardless of where the server is physically located — and a gag order means the company may never even know whether it happened. Navichain owns and operates its own Swedish servers and is therefore immune.

## Visual style (unchanged series standard)

Warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin *ligne claire* aesthetic. Clean, evenly weighted black outlines. Flat, even colour areas — no halftone screen, no heavy cross-hatching. Parchment/cream base with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold. Captions hand-lettered, not digital sans-serif. Physical props ahead of screens where possible (stamps, seals, clipboards, printed documents, cork noticeboards, Bakelite telephones) — but because this story is specifically about servers, clouds and digital architecture, panels 7–12 may exceptionally show a **retro CRT screen/control panel** as the main motif where required, framed by the same warm, patinated line drawing. Lightly grainy offset-print feel with age and patina — subtle paper texture, soft edge wear, muted/slightly faded colours, like a well-thumbed 1950s magazine from a drawer. The characters are warm, expressive and sympathetic; genuinely happy and smiling in the resolution panels.

**Colour dramaturgy:**

- Panels 1–6: Red → problem/crisis phase (the Uncle Sam shadow present)
- Transition: Orange/mustard → turning point
- Panels 7–12: Petroleum green → solution (Uncle Sam completely shut out, never visible again)

**Logo rules:**

- Always called **"Navichain's official logo"** — never "N logo", "N symbol", "N monogram", "N mark" or similar
- Completely absent from panels 1–6
- Introduced discreetly from panel 7 — as an app corner, coffee mug, sign, document watermark, clipboard etc.
- Maximum about 5% of the panel area, never centred or advertisement-like
- Navichain's official logo is uploaded as a source file in the project. The illustrator must use the uploaded file at every specified placement below — never an invented or stylised replacement

- Use the official Navichain logo file supplied separately — never invent your own variant.

- No IT support character appears — Sofia navigates Navichain's interface herself, without help, which is a deliberate point: the platform explains itself.
- Keep the colour dramaturgy strict: red (1–5), orange (6, turning point), green (7–12).
- Robban Ek must be drawn consistently with his established appearance from earlier stories in the series.
