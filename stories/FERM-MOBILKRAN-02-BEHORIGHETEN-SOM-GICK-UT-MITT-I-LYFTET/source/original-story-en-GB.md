## Product boundary

Navichain is used here as **DISPLAY / REPORTING** for driver validity and expiry dates. The system does not renew documents automatically, does not book a renewal course/test, and is not claimed to have a dedicated crane-operator-authorisation module. The person handles the renewal; Navichain makes the date visible.
