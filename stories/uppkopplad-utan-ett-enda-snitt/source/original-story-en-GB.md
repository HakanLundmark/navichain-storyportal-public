## Script, panel by panel

### Panel 1 — Red
**Image:** Dawn at the Ferm Mobilkran AB yard. Four mobile cranes are lined up. Mats stands by a whiteboard with magnetic notes — “Crane 1: ?”, “Crane 3: with Lasse, where?” — and a mobile phone already ringing in his hand.  
**Caption:** “Mats always knew where the cranes *should* be. Where they actually were was another matter.”

### Panel 2 — Red
**Image:** Construction site. Åsa Brolin stands with her arms folded, looks at the clock, then at the empty place where the crane should be.  
**Caption:** “The crane should have been here at seven.”

### Panel 3 — Red
**Image:** Mats in his office, phone to his ear, a missed-call icon visible on the screen. Through the window, an empty yard can be seen.  
**Caption:** “Lasse did not answer. No one knew whether the crane was stuck in traffic, refuelling or already on site.”

### Panel 4 — Red
**Image:** Sepia-toned flashback: Mats sits at the desk months earlier, looking at a quote on the screen. Figures for hardware, installation and a line: “Vehicle left at workshop, approx. 1 day/unit, intervention in electrical system.”  
**Caption:** “A technician had explained that the tracking unit would require the cranes to stand still for a full day — and that someone would cut into the electrical system near the hydraulics.”

### Panel 5 — Red
**Image:** Mats shakes his head and closes the laptop lid with some force. The whiteboard with magnetic notes is visible in the background as the only “solution”.  
**Caption:** “A day of downtime per crane, for an uncertain guarantee. Mats left it alone.”

### Panel 6 — Red
**Image:** The same construction site as panel 2, now with the crane finally in place — but Åsa is standing with a notepad, recording the time, clearly displeased. Mats is in the background, sweating, on the phone.  
**Caption:** “The crane arrived. Two hours late. Åsa wrote it down. Mats realised what not knowing was costing him.”

### Panel 7 — Orange (turning point)
**Image:** Mats at his desk in the evening, laptop open. On the screen is a webpage with a heading about a hardware subscription for tracking units — no large investment, no workshop drop-off. A small tab icon/logo is discreetly visible in the browser corner.  
**Caption:** “No purchase price. No workshop time. Just a subscription — and a unit that did not require anyone to cut a single cable.”

### Panel 8 — Green
**Image:** The yard in the morning. A small box containing four compact units (FMC003) is open on a toolbox. Lasse holds one of the units and plugs it in using quick connectors behind the dashboard — no screwdriver, no cut cable, no welding flame in sight. Mats stands beside him with folded arms, looking surprised and amused.  
**Caption:** “Fifteen minutes later the unit was in place. Not one crane left the yard.”

### Panel 9 — Green
**Image:** Mats holds a tablet showing a map with all four cranes as points — one moving, one stationary, clearly distinguished visually. In the background, as a small detail, Robban Ek's familiar truck passes on its way to the same construction site with a delivery of reinforcing steel; he raises a hand in greeting.  
**Caption:** “For the first time, Mats could see where all four cranes actually were — not just where they were supposed to be.”

### Panel 10 — Green
**Image:** The phone rings in the office. Åsa Brolin is on the other end, asking for a crane at short notice. Mats looks at the tablet, immediately sees which crane is nearest and available, points and confirms verbally within seconds.  
**Caption:** “This time Mats did not need to phone around. He already knew.”

### Panel 11 — Green
**Image:** Lasse stands by his crane in the field, looking over Mats's shoulder at his phone. An equipment view shows the crane's lifting equipment with an inspection date highlighted in amber — not urgent, but getting close.  
**Caption:** “The system warned him in good time. No last-minute surprise — and no one renewed the inspection for him. Mats still had to book it himself.”

### Panel 12 — Green
**Image:** Evening in the yard. Mats sits on a stool with a cup of coffee, the tablet in his lap showing all four cranes calmly positioned for the next morning. On the table beside him is a small note with a sketch of an authorisation card and an expiry date — a hint at the next problem, not a direct continuation of this episode.  
**Caption:** “Four cranes. One yard. And for the first time — no guessing game.”

## Continuity notes

- This is a standalone story, not a continuation of an earlier numbered thread.
- Robban Ek is used as an intended cross-series cameo and has no central function.
- No IT support function appears in any panel.
- Mats Ferm is the protagonist throughout the arc, from problem to solution.

## Next-episode teaser

The note in panel 12 hints at the next story: **“The authorisation that expired in the middle of the lift”**.

## Product terminology and grounding

- **Telematics registration:** documented Teltonika models explicitly include FMC003.
- **Fleet Map:** shows reporting vehicles and visually distinct status; updating is documented as polling approximately every 30 seconds.
- **Equipment:** status, value, maintenance and calibration; dates can be colour-coded but are not renewed automatically.
- **Truck/vehicle:** the mobile crane is represented as a vehicle with a linked telematics unit.

## Explicit boundary

- HaaS is the commercial framework in the story; hardware model and vehicle compatibility are configuration-dependent.
- No automatic dispatch or automatic booking is claimed — Mats makes the decision himself based on the map.
