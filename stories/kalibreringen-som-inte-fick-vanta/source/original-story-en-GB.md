## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-CAPABILITY-INDEX.md, NAVICHAIN-TERMINOLOGY.md):

- **Equipment:** Non-vehicle asset (tools/machines) with status, value, maintenance and **calibration**. The maintenance, warranty and calibration fields are displayed/reported (**DISPLAY / REPORTING**) — they are not performed automatically.
- **Equipment status:** Active, Maintenance, Retired, Out of Service — all **user-initiated**, not automatic.
- **Work order:** Covers a vehicle/a trailer/an equipment item, status, priority, type, assignment/workshop/date, time/cost, instructions and parts. Created by the user, or through the "Check Maintenance" scan.
- **Workshops:** Directory of internal/external/partner workshops with location, contact details and hourly rate — displayed/reported.
- **Equipment inspection:** Template-based check matched to equipment type, with status Clean/Issue Reported/Resolved.

**Explicit limitation (transparent pivot):**
- The calibration date is **not renewed automatically** by the system. The story explicitly shows that a real, completed action (a booked work order, calibration carried out at a workshop) is required before the field can be updated to a new date.
- No automatic link between a customer's booking and the equipment's calibration status is claimed — Mats and Lasse themselves discover and act on the clash between Henrik's job and the calibration date; it is not a system-generated warning that blocks the booking.
- There is no documented, named field specifically for a "calibration certificate for lifting equipment" in the underlying documentation. The next-episode teaser about "proof" is deliberately left open for further research against the document library before that story is written.

## Illustrator's production notes

- **Style:** The same warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin *ligne claire* as the previous episode — uniform contour lines, flat colour fields, parchment/cream base, rust red/mustard yellow/petroleum green/warm gold, hand-lettered captions, light grainy offset-print patina.
- **Colour dramaturgy especially clear in this story:** The calibration field's colour transition (yellow → red → green) is the central visual thread through the whole episode and should be drawn as a recurring, clearly recognisable element in panels 1, 6 and 10.
- **Physical props:** The wall calendar, the scribbled note and the coffee mug as physical objects in the problem section. Screens are kept simple and stylised in the solution section, not realistic UI screenshots.
- **No IT support character:** Navichain should appear self-explanatory — Mats and Lasse find and use the Equipment and work-order views themselves, without outside help.
- **Logo:** Use **Navichain's official logo** — it is uploaded as a source file in the project and should be used directly by the illustrator, not a stylised substitute. Never call it the "N logo", "N symbol" or "N monogram".
  - **Panels 1–6:** The logo is completely absent.
  - **Panel 7:** The logo is introduced discreetly, as an app header in the Equipment view.
  - **Panels 8–12:** The logo may appear discreetly as a prop — never centred or advertisement-like, max. about 5% of the panel area.
