# THE DELIVERY THAT GOT A SECOND CHANCE
## Robban and the door that was closed — but never the story

**Format:** 12 panels + cover concept.

**Angle:** The thirty-third episode in the series about connected workflows at Björklunda Åkeri AB gives **Robban Ek** the lead role and resolves the teaser from episode 32: a failed delivery does not have to mean that everything starts again from scratch. Robban arrives at an address where no one is home. In the present-day workflow, he records the failed delivery with a reason in the mobile app. At the office, **Sanna Berg** sees what happened and explicitly chooses to create a **reattempt**. Sanna then chooses the new delivery date herself and plans the next run. Navichain does not make the decision for her.

**Product Truth boundary:** Failure reason is documented in the mobile app. Reattempt is a user-initiated action. The story does not claim autonomous replanning and makes no specific claim about exact automatic field inheritance that has not been verified.

## Cast

- **Robban Ek** — driver, Björklunda Åkeri AB; protagonist.
- **Sanna Berg** — planning manager, Björklunda Åkeri AB; brief cameo in the solution workflow.

## Cover concept

Robban at a closed door, the parcel still in his arms, looking at the mobile phone in his other hand rather than at a notepad. Behind him: an old abandoned pile of paper symbolising the previous way of working.

## PANEL 1 — Robban at a closed door
Robban is standing at a company entrance with a parcel in his arms. The door is locked and no one answers.

**Text:** "Right address. Right time. Apparently the wrong day for the customer."

## PANEL 2 — The old way: phone call to the office
Robban calls the office from the truck and leafs through a binder with booking details.

**Text:** "He used to read the entire booking over the phone, word for word."

## PANEL 3 — A new booking is entered by hand
At the office, the same information is entered again from scratch.

**Text:** "A new booking. The same details. Written all over again, as if they had never existed."

## PANEL 4 — Robban waits in the cab
Robban remains in the driver's cab with the parcel beside him and waits for instructions.

**Text:** "One parcel that could not be delivered. And a driver who could only wait."

## PANEL 5 — The next day, the same uncertainty
Another driver arrives at the same address without the context from the first attempt.

**Text:** "No one driving the second time knew what had happened the first time."

## PANEL 6 — The customer, just as confused as the drivers
The customer opens the door, surprised, and lacks the context around the previous attempt.

**Text:** "No one really knew what had happened. Least of all the customer."

## PANEL 7 — Robban opens the mobile app at the door
Back in the present, Robban opens the current booking in the mobile app at the closed door.

**Text:** "No phone binder. Just the booking, already in his hand."

## PANEL 8 — The reason is recorded on the spot
Robban records the reason **"No one on site"** in the failure workflow.

**Text:** "One reason, selected on the spot. No one had to explain it twice."

## PANEL 9 — The failure is already there
The booking shows the recorded reason. The parcel is still with Robban.

**Text:** "The reason stayed with the booking. No one had to start with a blank sheet."

## PANEL 10 — Sanna opens the failed delivery
Sanna opens the existing booking, sees the delivery exception and explicitly chooses to create a reattempt.

**Text:** "Sanna saw what had happened — and chose to give the delivery another attempt."

## PANEL 11 — The reattempt is planned
Sanna chooses the new delivery date herself and plans the reattempt on an upcoming run.

**Text:** "She chose the date. The system only kept track of the rest."

## PANEL 12 — Robban, same address, second time
Robban is back at the same door. This time, the customer meets him and receives the parcel.

**Text:** "Same delivery. A new attempt. Never a new story."

Sanna makes the decision about the reattempt and the new date; Navichain functions as the connected tool, not as an autonomous decision-maker.
