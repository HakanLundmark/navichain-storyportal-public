# THE WARNING THAT CAME BEFORE THE CHOICE
### A Navichain story — Yvonne, Rasmus and the assignment that almost went wrong again

**Persona:** Yvonne Kron, 44, coordinator/dispatcher, Kron Elteknik AB  
**Co-protagonist:** Rasmus Öberg, 28, field technician, Kron Elteknik AB  
**Supporting character:** Daniel Ohls (cameo), and Rolf Ingman, inspector (returning from “The Stamp That Was About to Fade”)  
**Persona thread:** Episode 10 in the Kron Elteknik thread — a deeper look at the same area as episode 9: personal authorisation with an expiry date  
**Theme:** Seeing a date on a profile is not enough if nobody looks at it at the right moment — the authorisation must be visible *when the technician is actually being chosen*, not only when someone happens to open the profile  
**Navichain’s role in the story:** The assignment view that already shows a “busy” marker (established in episode 4) also shows a compliance/authorisation marker — so that a technician’s valid or invalid certification is visible at the same moment the choice is made, not afterwards

**Format:** 12 panels + cover concept, Swedish dialogue/captions in the source master, warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin-style ligne claire, parchment/cream base palette with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold, hand-drawn captions, discreet print grain and patina — modern context, classic visual language  
**Colour dramaturgy:** 🔴 Red (problem, panels 1–6) → 🟠 Orange (turning point, panel 7) → 🟢 Petroleum green (solution, panels 8–12)

**Logo rule (mandatory):** Navichain’s official logo is completely absent from panels 1–6. It is introduced discreetly from panel 7 onwards — as an environmental detail (the app header on a mobile screen, a sticker on the service van), never centred or advertisement-like, maximum approx. 5% of the panel area.
