## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-CAPABILITY-INDEX.md, NAVICHAIN-TERMINOLOGY.md):

- **Draft PO at low level (established in episode 2):** "Order" creates a draft purchase order to replenish to a set minimum level. Source: Product Truth §9 ("Order creates a draft PO to replenish the minimum").
- **PO receiving:** Purchase orders move Draft → Sent → Confirmed → Partially Received → Received → Closed; receipt (partial or full) updates stock balance and writes a receipt movement. Source: Product Truth §18 (Status Models — Purchase order) and Capability Index #95.
- **Booking overview as planning input:** Upcoming bookings for multiple technicians can be viewed together, which is what Micke uses to compare needs before allocating a newly arrived delivery. Source: the same booking basis as earlier episodes (Terminology — core entity map).
- **Reasoned relocation between locations:** Just as in episode 7, allocation between the main warehouse and each service van takes place as reasoned adjustments to the Parts balance, not as a dedicated "Transfer" function. Source: Product Truth §9.

**Explicit boundary (what is NOT claimed):** The story does not claim that the system automatically prioritises or allocates an incoming delivery between technicians based on the urgency of bookings — Micke actively compares and decides. Nor does it claim that the express-freight scenario itself is a Navichain function; it depicts a real consequence of poor planning, which the solution then prevents.
