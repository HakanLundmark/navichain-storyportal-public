# QUIET IN THE CAB

### Blixten's night through the storm

**— A navichain story —**

**Character:** Johan "Blixten" Holm (35), long-haul driver, Nordsvenska Tungtransporter AB **Supporting character:** Maria, dispatcher at the office in Skellefteå **Format:** 12 panels, illustrator's script **Context:** Modern day — but the visual language should feel like a well-thumbed 1950s album

---

## Visual style (applies to all panels)

**Base style:** Warm 1950s aesthetics in Hergé/Tintin "ligne claire" — clean contour lines with even line weight, flat/even colour areas, minimal halftone shading, no heavy cross-hatching.

**Texture:** Consistent grainy offset patina — like a well-thumbed period magazine: subtle print grain, slight paper texture, soft edge wear/foxing, muted/slightly faded tones.

**Palette:** Parchment/cream base surface with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold.

**Colour dramaturgy:** Red (danger/chaos) → orange/mustard (transition/insight) → petroleum green (solution). Follows the status marker in each panel.

**Text treatment:** Captions are rendered handwritten/hand-lettered — not digital sans-serif.

**Props:** Physical objects (consignment note, clipboard, stamp, road sign, coffee mug) are preferred to screenshots. Where a phone or instrument-panel screen is necessary to the story (panels 5, 6, 8), it should be subordinate to the physical scene — Johan's face, hands, the weather, the road — rather than dominate the image area. Retro-like screens (warm, simple interfaces) are preferred to sharp modern UI mock-ups.

**Character:** Johan should read as warm and genuinely expressive. In the solution panels (especially 10–12), he should show genuine relief and joy — not merely neutral relaxation.

---

## Logo instruction (applies throughout)

**navichain's official logo** means navichain's actual official brand logo — not a generic "N" symbol or an original interpretation. The illustrator should use the official logo file uploaded as a source file in the project, placed exactly as instructed in each panel.

- **Panels 1–6:** The logo is completely absent. This is the problem phase — stress, distraction, the old way of working.
- **Panels 7–12:** The logo is introduced discreetly as an environmental detail (app icon on the phone, decal on the truck door, mug in the office, small label on the clipboard). Never more than about 5% of the panel area, never centred or advertising-like.

### Panel 1 — The order

**Image:** The office in Skellefteå, late evening. Maria calls Johan while looking at a weather map showing a severe snowstorm over the inland route. Johan answers in his truck at the loading bay; snow has just started to fall. **Caption:** "Johan, the weather is bad on the inland route tonight. 30 tonnes of steel, and Sundsvall expects delivery at seven tomorrow morning." **Status:** 🔴 Red — the storm is moving in

---

### Panel 2 — How it used to be

**Image:** A short, slightly more muted "memory panel": an older version of Johan's routine — he leafs through paper slips on the passenger seat while the phone rings uncontrollably, his eyes leaving the slippery road for a moment. **Caption:** "Back then, the phone rang every thirty minutes. Paper on his lap, eyes off the road at exactly the wrong second." **Status:** 🔴 Red — distraction was part of the job

---

### Panel 3 — A new way of working

**Image:** Johan in the cab before departure, holding up his phone and looking at it with amused scepticism. No manual in his hand, no IT technician in the image — just him and the screen. **Caption:** "Nordsvenska has introduced navichain's driver app. No manual, no IT support to call. It should just work." **Status:** 🟠 Orange — a new tool is tried

---

### Panel 4 — The safety walk-around goes digital

**Image:** Johan walks around the truck in the snowfall with the phone in his hand. A simple checklist is visible on the screen: brakes, tyres, lighting, load securing. He photographs a loose restraint with the phone camera. **Caption:** "Before the wheels even start rolling: a digital safety walk-around. Five button presses, no paper form to lose." **Status:** 🟠 Orange — control before departure

---

### Panel 5 — Quiet in the cab

**Image:** The truck rolls out into the storm. We see Johan's profile, focused on the road. The phone sits in a holder, the screen simple and calm; a discreet sound-wave symbol shows that a message is being read aloud — not displayed. **Caption:** "As soon as the wheels start rolling, the app locks into driving mode. Messages are read aloud. Johan never touches the screen." **Status:** 🟠 Orange — focus on the road

---

### Panel 6 — The road that wasn't on the map

**Image:** A closed road ahead; an old bridge with a sign indicating limited load capacity is visible in the darkness. Instead, Johan follows an alternative, gritted road that is indicated — physical road sign in the foreground, no screen in the image. **Caption:** "A bridge with too low a weight limit. A normal map would have led him straight there. This one didn't." **Status:** 🟠 Orange — the right road, automatically

---

### Panel 7 — The tyre failure

**Image:** The truck is stationary on a dark bend, a blown trailer tyre. Johan scratches his head and presses one clear button on the phone. A small part of navichain's official logo is visible discreetly as an app icon in the corner of the phone. **Caption:** "A trailer tyre blows in the dark. One tap on the screen — that's all it takes." **Status:** 🟠 Orange — help requested

---

### Panel 8 — The alert gets through

**Image:** Split panel: the office in Skellefteå, Maria at her desk sees a silent notification appear on the screen with exact coordinates and vehicle details — no ringtone, no stressful call. A navichain mug stands beside her. **Caption:** "No call, no alarm in the handset. Just the exact position and the right information, straight on the screen." **Status:** 🟠 Orange — the organisation knows

---

### Panel 9 — The recovery truck finds him directly

**Image:** A recovery truck arrives at exactly the right place in the snowstorm. No searching along the roadside, no confusion. A small navichain sticker is visible discreetly on the recovery driver's jacket or vehicle. **Caption:** "No guessing game about where the truck was. The wheel was changed in under an hour." **Status:** 🟢 Green — solved quickly

---

### Panel 10 — The road continues

**Image:** Johan back behind the wheel, the storm beginning to ease, dawn light visible on the horizon. His face shows genuine relief, almost a smile. The clipboard on the passenger seat has a small navichain label. **Caption:** "Still tired. But never alone, and never unsure of where he was." **Status:** 🟢 Green — a safe journey

---

### Panel 11 — Arriving in Sundsvall

**Image:** Dawn, 06:15. Johan drives into the printing company's yard and brings up a digital delivery confirmation on the phone with a single tap — no paper to fill in or hand over. A small navichain decal is visible discreetly on the truck door. **Caption:** "06:15. One tap, and the delivery is confirmed. No form, no paper that can blow away in the wind." **Status:** 🟢 Green — done and documented

---

### Panel 12 — Home, and the next alert is waiting

**Image:** Johan steps through the door at home and is met by his family — a warm, genuine smile. In the background, as a small transition detail, Maria's office is visible through a window in the corner of the panel: she leans towards a ringing phone with a new case on the screen, ready for the next story. **Caption:** "Blixten is home. And at the office, the next story is already waiting — the one about the load that almost ended up in the wrong truck." **Status:** 🟢 Green — the night is over

## Summary note to the illustrator

- **navichain's official logo** = the official brand logo (uploaded as a source file in the project), never an original "N" interpretation.
- The logo is completely absent in panels 1–6, discreetly introduced from panel 7 (app icon, decal, mug, label) — max about 5% of the panel area, never central or advertising-like.
- Johan should be visually and emotionally consistent throughout all 12 panels: the same face, the same clothing, the same warm expression that deepens towards the end.
- Keep physical props in focus even in the panels where a screen is necessary (5, 6, 8) — the screen should support the scene, not dominate it.
- The final panel contains a clear teaser towards the next instalment in the series (the package that almost ended up in the wrong truck).
