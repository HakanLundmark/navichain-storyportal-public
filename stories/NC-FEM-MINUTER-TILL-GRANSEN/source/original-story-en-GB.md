# A NAVICHAIN STORY

## Five minutes to the limit

### Karin, Robban and the alert that arrived in time

**Story ID:** `NC-FEM-MINUTER-TILL-GRANSEN`  
**Source locale:** `sv-SE`  
**Locale:** `en-GB`  
**Translation type:** faithful  
**Narrative Master:** false  
**Master version:** not explicitly versioned in the chat

**Format:** 12-panel illustrated storyboard / illustrator brief.  
**Persona:** Karin Ström, 46, dispatcher at Norrgläntans Åkeri AB (18 vehicles, long-haul operations in central Sweden).  
**Recurring character:** Robban Ek, driver.  
**Product angle in the original brief:** Driver app + HaaS telematics (Teltonika FMC650), driving/rest-time data and a proactive alert before an infringement.

## Visual direction

Warm classic European `ligne claire` aesthetics with clean outlines, flat colour fields, cream/parchment, rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold. The setting is the modern day with modern European trucks, phones, computers and digital flows. The problem phase is red, the turning point orange/mustard and the solution phase petroleum green. The Navichain logo is absent from panels 1–6 and introduced discreetly from panel 7.

## Panel 01

**Image:** Karin's office, early morning. She is sitting with an opened brown envelope in front of her. Her face is rigid. A wall calendar has yesterday's date circled in red.  
**Locked caption after preflight:** "A new sanction case. A driver had driven too long — and Karin only found out after the inspection."  
**Status:** Red — sanction after a deviation.

## Panel 02

**Image:** Flashback, the previous day: Robban Ek behind the wheel in the rain, eyes on the road, a stack of paper driving schedules on the passenger seat. No clock or meter shows his remaining driving time.  
**Caption:** "Robban didn't know how much time he had left. Nobody knew. It wasn't written on any paper he had in the cab."  
**Status:** Red — no real-time information about driving time.

## Panel 03

**Image:** Karin in the office with a telephone in her hand and a list of drivers' names on a notepad. She is calling around; one row has an uncertain question mark beside it.  
**Caption:** "She called to ask how much driving time was left. Half the fleet didn't answer."  
**Status:** Red — manual reconciliation, incomplete coverage.

## Panel 04

**Image:** Karin puts down the envelope, leans back, and a pile of similar old cases sits in a basket labelled "DEVIATIONS".  
**Caption:** "It was the third time in a year. Not because the drivers were careless — but because nobody saw it in time."  
**Status:** Red — pattern of deviations discovered too late.

## Panel 05

**Image:** Karin at her desk with a brochure about the Teltonika FMC650. A colleague points to the subscription/hardware information.  
**Caption:** "No investment to justify to the board. Just a subscription — and a workshop appointment."  
**Status:** Orange — the decision is made.

## Panel 06

**Image:** A workshop mechanic installs a small telematics unit beneath the dashboard of one of Norrgläntans' trucks. The installation looks compact and contained.  
**Locked caption after preflight:** "One workshop, one hour, once. No separate system integration to build. Once the unit is installed, the data goes straight into Navichain."  
**Status:** Orange — hardware is installed.

## Panel 07

**Image:** Red → green transition in the same truck cab: before the active data flow versus after the telematics is connected and data is displayed. A discreet Navichain logo is introduced here.  
**Locked caption after preflight:** "As soon as the telematics is connected and the data flow is active — Karin sees the fleet in a completely new way."  
**Status:** Red → green — data starts to flow.

## Panel 08

**Image:** Karin's office, warmly lit. A rounded monitor shows a simple fleet overview with vehicles and today's driving-time status.  
**Caption:** "No more guessing. Every vehicle, every driver's driving time — on one screen, updated in real time."  
**Status:** Green — overview.

## Panel 09

**Image:** Close-up of the overview: Robban is marked with about half an hour of driving time remaining. Karin leans forward, attentive but calm.  
**Caption:** "Robban has half an hour left. This time Karin knows before he does."  
**Status:** Green — risk detected in advance.

## Panel 10

**Image:** Karin adds a new stop/rest option to Robban's route and sends the information to the driver app.  
**Caption:** "One swipe of a finger. Robban gets a new, legal stop before he has even had to wonder where to take his break."  
**Status:** Green — rerouting sent.

## Panel 11

**Image:** Robban in the cab. The phone in its holder shows a new stop in 4 km and a 45-minute rest period. He drives calmly towards the exit.  
**Caption:** "No stress. No clock he has to keep track of himself. The app kept track — he just drove."  
**Status:** Green — the driver receives the right information in time.

## Panel 12

**Image:** Karin's office, late afternoon. She leans back with her coffee mug; the screen reads "0 deviations today" and the "DEVIATIONS" basket is empty.  
**Locked caption after preflight:** "No new driving-time case today. Just the fleet, the driving times — and a dispatcher who sees the risks while she can still do something about them."  
**Status:** Green — control.

**Teaser:** "Next story: Sofia needs to send a quote within the hour — but the price list is in three different binders…"

## Continuity

Robban Ek: dark cap, blue jacket. Karin Ström: short dark hair, practical office clothes, tired/pressured at the beginning and calmer towards the end. Navichain is the tool; Karin and Robban are the people driving the story.
