# 90 days ahead — The competence register that saved the million deal

**Story ID:** `NC-90-DAGARS-FORSPRANG`  
**Master locale:** `sv-SE`  
**Master version:** `1.0`  
**Protagonist:** Susanne Wikström, 47 — Quality and HR Manager, Vindelälvens Åkeri AB (22 vehicles, refrigerated and ADR-classified food transports in northern Sweden)  
**Supporting character:** Robert “Robban” Ek — driver, recurring series character  
**Supporting character:** Mrs Ahlberg — procurement manager, Norrlands Livs & Grossist AB  
**Source/angle:** Resource management as a business opportunity: documented competence, SOP read acknowledgement and audit readiness.

## Locked preflight corrections

- Panels 3/8: Robban’s ADR validity date is `2026-08-05`, so that “expired six days ago” is chronologically consistent with the story’s Tuesday, 11 August 2026.
- Panel 9: the YKB example is `2026-11-09`, exactly 90 days after 11 August 2026.

**Caption:** “Susanne, we need your competence register for the entire fleet. Complete, with expiry dates. We want it on the desk on Thursday.” — Mrs Ahlberg, Norrlands Livs & Grossist AB

**Image:** Susanne hangs up and stares at the binder shelf. She pulls out a binder labelled “ADR — GILTIGHETSDATUM” and flips through it frantically. Several loose sheets fall to the floor.  
**Caption:** 22 vehicles. 31 drivers. Each with YKB, ADR, perhaps a forklift licence. No consolidated list — just this.  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — scattered information

**Image:** Close-up of a handwritten Excel-like sheet with columns for names and dates, several rows crossed out and rewritten by hand in the margin. One date is heavily underlined: “R. EK — ADR — 2026-08-05”.  
**Caption:** One date stands out. Robban’s ADR certificate. Has it already expired?  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — uncertainty

## Panel 4

**Image:** Susanne calls Robban. Robban is standing by his truck at a rest area, looking for a folded, worn certificate in his inside pocket/wallet.  
**Caption:** “Wait, I have it in my wallet somewhere... or is it in the glove compartment?”  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — no traceable original

## Panel 5

**Image:** Susanne sits with her head in her hands at the desk; the clock shows late afternoon. In front of her is a makeshift sheet with only seven of thirty-one names filled in and several question marks.  
**Caption:** Half the day gone. Seven out of thirty-one checked. And no proven SOP reading to show if the auditor asks.  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — the tender is slipping away

## Panel 6

**Image:** Susanne stands up, pushes the pile of papers aside and opens her laptop. The screen glows warmly in the dimming office.  
**Caption:** “Binders and wallets are no longer enough.” Susanne decides: every driver, every certificate, must have its own digital identity.  
**Status:** 🟠 Orange — turning point

## Panel 7

**Image:** Susanne at the computer. A digital driver register with a row for every employee is visible on the screen. She uploads a scanned certificate with her mobile phone. The Navichain logo is discreetly visible in the corner of the screen.  
**Caption:** She builds the register in one afternoon — not thirty-one binder sheets, but a list that keeps track of its own dates.  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — register being built

## Panel 8

**Image:** The screen shows Robban’s profile: photo, YKB date, ADR date and a clear red warning: “ADR — EXPIRED 6 DAYS AGO”. Susanne raises her eyebrows, surprised but relieved to know.  
**Caption:** The truth, finally visible: Robban’s ADR certificate really did expire six days ago. Nobody knew. Now everyone knows.  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — risks visible

## Panel 9

**Image:** Close-up of Susanne’s mobile phone with a notification: “90-day reminder: M. Sundin, YKB expires 2026-11-09”. In the background, Robban books a renewal course on his own phone, smiling and relaxed.  
**Caption:** Now the system sends the warning 90, 60 and 30 days in advance — to Susanne and to the driver. Nobody has to remember everything any more.  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — automatic monitoring

## Panel 10

**Image:** Susanne uploads the haulage company’s SOP for load securing and health checks in Navichain. The drivers’ phones receive a notification and they confirm that they have read and understood it; the acknowledgement is time-stamped.  
**Caption:** The procedures are distributed digitally. Every read acknowledgement is time-stamped and saved — audit-ready from the first second.  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — documented knowledge

## Panel 11

**Image:** Wednesday evening. Susanne selects “Exportera rapport”. A complete report is generated with all 31 drivers’ certificates, expiry dates and SOP acknowledgements.  
**Caption:** Wednesday evening, one click. The entire competence register, exported and complete — a day before the deadline.  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — audit-ready

## Panel 12

**Image:** Thursday. Susanne sits opposite Mrs Ahlberg and hands over the report. Mrs Ahlberg leafs through it, impressed, and nods. A note hints at the next story: “Next: the vehicle’s ADR equipment — signs, fire extinguishers, eyewash.”  
**Caption:** “This is the most complete documentation we have seen from a subcontractor.” Susanne smiles. The million deal is secured — and next time it will not take an entire day.
