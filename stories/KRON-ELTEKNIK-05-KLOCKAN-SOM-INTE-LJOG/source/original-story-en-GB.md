## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-WORKFLOWS.md, NAVICHAIN-TERMINOLOGY.md):

- **Mobile proof with automatic timestamp:** Photos/signatures/comments are recorded by the technician, with GPS and time automatically added to each such registration. Source: Capability Index #128 ("Mobile proof photos/signature plus automatic GPS/time"); Terminology — "Pickup proof" / "POD".
- **Charges on the booking:** Charge lines on a booking can be added manually or through the pricing engine, which supports, among other things, a Time-based pricing type (price per unit of time). Source: Product Truth §11 (Finance/Pricing) — "Price sheet | Calculation rules for one price type: Weight, Volume, Distance, Time, Pallets or Load Meters."
- **Charge vs. Cost:** The charge is the customer-facing revenue line, separate from internal costs — which in the story corresponds to the documented time (not a standard estimate) being the basis for what the customer is invoiced. Source: Terminology — "Charge / Cost".

**Explicit boundary (what is NOT being claimed):** The story does not claim that there is a dedicated clock-in/clock-out function ("time clock") for technicians in the mobile app. What is depicted is that the photo/signature-based proof points already present in the flow (established in episode 1) carry automatic timestamps, and that these points in time — rather than a verbal estimate — form the basis when the charge line is entered. The charge line itself is entered by a user (Siv), not automatically calculated solely from the photo timestamps without human input.
