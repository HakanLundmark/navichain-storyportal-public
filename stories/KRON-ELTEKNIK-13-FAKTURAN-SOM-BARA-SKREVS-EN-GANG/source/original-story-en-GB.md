## Product terminology and grounding
- An issued invoice or credit note can be sent to a configured/supported accounting connection through an explicit user-initiated action.
- The story does not claim automatic background synchronisation.
- Cancelling an issued invoice creates a separate credit note in Navichain; an already-pushed entry in external accounting is not automatically credited there.
- Availability is configuration-dependent and requires the provider to be connected for the tenant.
