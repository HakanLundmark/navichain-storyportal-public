# THE STAMP THAT WAS ABOUT TO FADE
### A Navichain story — Daniel, Micke and the qualification no one saw expire

**Persona:** Daniel Ohls, 31, field technician, Kron Elteknik AB
**Co-protagonist:** Micke Sandin, 52, warehouse and purchasing manager (with dual responsibility for some administration), Kron Elteknik AB
**Supporting character:** Yvonne Kron (cameo), plus an inspector from a larger property customer, Rolf Ingman
**Persona thread:** Episode 9 in the Kron Elteknik thread — after “Packed Before the Wheels Rolled” and “The Right Part to the Right Van”
**Theme:** Personal qualification/certification with an expiry date — from an old piece of paper in a binder to a visible, colour-coded date that no one can miss any more

**Note on the previous teaser:** The previous episode teased “customer reviews” as the next angle. After checking the product documentation, there is no verified customer review/rating feature in Navichain, so that episode could not be grounded honestly. This episode therefore takes a different, well-grounded angle instead: visible expiry dates for personal qualifications.

**Format:** 12 panels + cover concept, Swedish dialogue/captions, warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin style (ligne claire), parchment/cream base palette with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold, hand-drawn captions, subtle print grain and patina — modern context, classic visual language
**Colour dramaturgy:** 🔴 Red (problem, panels 1–6) → 🟠 Orange (turning point, panel 7) → 🟢 Petroleum green (solution, panels 8–12)

**Logo rule (mandatory):** Navichain’s official logo is completely absent from panels 1–6. It is introduced discreetly from panel 7 onwards — as an environmental detail (mobile screen app header, a profile page on screen), never centred or advertising-like, max approx. 5% of the panel area. ## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-CAPABILITY-INDEX.md, NAVICHAIN-TERMINOLOGY.md):

- **Personnel profile with licence/certification:** A personnel profile (“Driver” in the core documentation) has fields for licence/certifications/shift, separate from the login account itself. Source: Terminology — Fleet/telematics/maintenance glossary (“Driver | Personnel profile with licence/certifications/shift; separate from User login”).
- **Colour-coded expiry dates:** Expiry dates for vehicle- and driver/personnel-related compliance are colour-coded and surfaced in the system. Source: Product Truth §26 (marketing boundaries table) — “Fleet compliance dates | Expiry dates are color-coded and surfaced | DISPLAY / REPORTING.”
- **Marketing boundary (explicit in the source):** The correct, approved wording is “Keep fleet and driver expiry dates visible” — NOT “Renews documents automatically.” The story follows this boundary strictly: Navichain displays and warns, but never renews Daniel’s qualification for him.

**Explicit limitation (what is NOT claimed):** The story does not claim that the system automatically renews or submits a renewed qualification on the user’s behalf — Daniel attends a course/actively renews it himself. Nor does it claim that the certification field is specifically built for electrical authorisation or any other named industry certification; it is the general licence/certification field on the personnel profile depicted in the story. In this story, Kron Elteknik’s field technicians, as in earlier episodes, are represented by the same personnel-profile entity that applies to drivers in the core documentation.
