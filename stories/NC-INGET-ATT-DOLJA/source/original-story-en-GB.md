# NOTHING TO HIDE

### A navichain story — Åsa & the customer portal

**Character:** Åsa Berndtsson, 47 — CEO and Head of Sales, Vänerfrakt AB (a haulage company with around 25 vehicles, mainly daily distribution assignments for a small number of large contract customers)  
**Supporting character:** Mats Öhman, Logistics Manager at Nordlivs (a grocery chain, one of Vänerfrakt's largest customers — accounting for a significant share of revenue)  
**Format:** 12-panel illustrator brief, using the same conventions as the other navichain stories  
**Colour coding:** 🔴 Red = trust crisis/lack of visibility, 🟠 Orange = insight/solution taking shape, 🟢 Green = trust regained

**Angle:** Unlike earlier customer-portal stories (which were about relieving customer service of individual tracking calls), this story is built around **the business value to the haulage company of being able to offer the customer a consolidated overview of ALL ongoing and completed jobs** — as a competitive advantage and a way to save the contract, not as a way to reduce phone calls.

**Visual style note (applies to all panels):** Heritage 1958 / Hergé-*ligne claire*: clean contour lines of even thickness, flat colour fields, parchment/cream base colour, rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green, warm gold. Hand-lettered captions. Grainy offset texture with soft patina. Contemporary action (laptops, mobile phones, digital dashboards) depicted consistently in this warm, classic visual language.

**navichain logo:**
- Absent in panels 1–6
- Introduced discreetly from panel 7: screen header, Åsa's coffee mug, possibly a small logo on a printed presentation
- Never more than around 5% of the panel area, never centred or advertising-like
- The official logo file must be used, with no reinterpretation

### Panel 1
**Setting:** Conference room at Vänerfrakt AB, contract meeting with Nordlivs.  
**Image:** Åsa sits opposite Mats Öhman at a table. Mats leafs through a binder and gently shakes his head.  
**Caption:** "'We've received an offer from another haulage company. They can show us the status of every job, in real time, whenever we want. You can't.'"  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — the contract is being questioned

### Panel 2
**Setting:** Same room, Åsa trying to respond, slightly sweaty.  
**Image:** She gives a verbal assurance that everything is being handled, but has nothing to show on the screen or on paper — only a promise.  
**Caption:** "Åsa can only promise. She cannot show. The difference can be felt in the room."  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — promises are no longer enough

### Panel 3
**Setting:** Vänerfrakt's office, the next day, Åsa at her computer with a long list of ongoing assignments for Nordlivs — spread across several different views and systems.  
**Image:** She manually copies and pastes status information into an Excel document, row by row.  
**Caption:** "A weekly report sheet, assembled by hand — the same work, every week, for a single customer."  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — manual extra work

### Panel 4
**Setting:** Åsa's office, the phone rings.  
**Image:** Mats Öhman on the other end of the line, irritated: a delivery was delayed and Nordlivs found out from its own store — not from Vänerfrakt.  
**Caption:** "'We found out about the delay from our store manager. Not from you. That's not okay, Åsa.'"  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — trust is being eroded

### Panel 5
**Setting:** Vänerfrakt's office, internal meeting, Åsa and a colleague.  
**Image:** Åsa with her arms crossed, looking worried. On the whiteboard: "Nordlivs — 22% of revenue" underlined.  
**Caption:** "If Nordlivs moves to a competitor, working harder will not be enough — Åsa has to be able to show more."  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — realising what is required

### Panel 6
**Setting:** Åsa's office, late evening, she is reading something on her computer.  
**Image:** A colleague walks past with a coffee cup and nods towards the screen, where navichain's customer portal feature is described.  
**Caption:** "'You don't have to build anything yourself — you simply invite Nordlivs into navichain. They can see everything, straight away.'"  
**Status:** 🟠 Orange — the insight lands

### Panel 7
**Setting:** Åsa's office, laptop open.  
**[Logo introduced here]**  
**Image:** Åsa creates a customer login for Nordlivs in the navichain interface. A discreet navichain logo appears in the screen header.  
**Caption:** "A couple of clicks. No in-house development, no IT project — just an invitation."  
**Illustrator's note:** Place the Navichain logo discreetly in the screen header, max around 5% of the panel area. Official logo file.  
**Status:** 🟠 Orange — the portal is set up

### Panel 8
**Setting:** The screen, Nordlivs's customer portal view.  
**Image:** A consolidated dashboard shows all of Nordlivs's ongoing and completed jobs — status, delivery times, deviations — on a single screen, rather than spread across different systems.  
**Caption:** "Not one link per delivery. A single view — of everything Vänerfrakt does for Nordlivs."  
**Status:** 🟠 Orange — full overview takes shape

### Panel 9
**Setting:** Mats Öhman's office at Nordlivs.  
**Image:** Mats logs in on his own screen for the first time and sees the whole week's deliveries in a list with clear status — completed, ongoing, delayed with the reason stated.  
**Caption:** "No waiting for an answer. Mats can see it himself — everything, at the same time."  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — the customer gains control

### Panel 10
**Setting:** New contract meeting, the same conference room as in panel 1.  
**Image:** Åsa turns her laptop towards Mats and shows the portal live instead of making a verbal promise. Mats leans forward, interested.  
**Caption:** "This time Åsa does not need to promise. She shows."  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — trust is regained

### Panel 11
**Setting:** Vänerfrakt's office, a few weeks later.  
**Image:** Åsa and a colleague go through new sales material in which the customer portal is mentioned as part of the offer to a new prospective customer.  
**Caption:** "What saved the Nordlivs contract has become something Åsa now sells from the start."  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — competitive advantage

### Panel 12
**Setting:** Åsa's office, late afternoon, warm light.  
**Image:** Åsa leans back with the coffee mug (navichain logo) in her hand. The screen behind her shows the Nordlivs dashboard, everything green. The contract binder from panel 1 is closed and put away.  
**Caption:** "Nothing to hide. Nothing to promise. Just to show."  
**Teaser box:** "Next: the driver whose customer saw the delivery being delivered — before the driver had even had time to sit down in the cab again."  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — the result

## Production notes for the illustrator
**Logo treatment:** the navichain logo is absent in panels 1–6 and introduced discreetly from panel 7 (screen header, coffee mug). Always use the official logo file.

**Colour arc:** Red (trust crisis, manual extra work, contract at risk) → Orange (portal is set up) → Green (customer gets its own visibility, contract is saved, becomes a competitive advantage).

**Continuity:** Åsa Berndtsson (CEO/Head of Sales, Vänerfrakt AB) and Mats Öhman (Logistics Manager, Nordlivs) are new personas — with no previous connection to the existing role list.

**Angle/boundary against earlier customer-portal stories:** This story focuses on **business value and contract retention** through a consolidated multi-job overview for the customer — unlike the Sofia story (single live ETA link to relieve customer service) and the Fredrik story (the transport buyer's own booking portal across several haulage companies).
