# PACKED BEFORE THE WHEELS STARTED ROLLING
### A navichain story — Micke, Daniel and the evening that saved the morning

**Persona:** Micke Sandin, 52, warehouse and purchasing manager, Kron Elteknik AB
**Co-protagonist:** Daniel Ohls, 31, field technician, Kron Elteknik AB
**Supporting character:** Yvonne Kron (cameo), and customer Elsa Furuvik
**Persona thread:** Episode 7 in the Kron Elteknik thread — builds on the Parts location field introduced in "The return trip that never needed to happen" (episode 2)
**Theme:** Spare-parts stock in the service van, part 1 — picking and preparing the right parts for a known, planned job *before* the technician even gets into the van
**Navichain's role in the story:** The Parts register's location field allows stock to be moved between the main warehouse and a specific service van in advance, with a traceable reason for each move — so the van is already packed correctly when the job starts

**Note to the reader:** This is the first of two connected episodes about spare-parts stock in the field. Part 2 ("The right part for the right van") continues where this episode ends and goes deeper into how replenishment is allocated sensibly across several vans.

**Format:** 12 panels + cover concept, Swedish dialogue/captions in the master, warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin style (ligne claire), parchment/cream base palette with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold, hand-drawn captions, subtle print grain and patina — modern context, classic visual language
**Colour dramaturgy:** 🔴 Red (problem, panels 1–6) → 🟠 Orange (turning point, panel 7) → 🟢 Petroleum green (solution, panels 8–12)

**Logo rule (mandatory):** navichain's official logo is completely absent in panels 1–6. It is introduced discreetly from panel 7 onwards — as an environmental detail (app header on a mobile screen, a shelf label in the warehouse), never centred or advertising-like, maximum approx. 5% of the panel area. The illustrator must use the uploaded source file for navichain's official logo.

## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-CAPABILITY-INDEX.md, NAVICHAIN-TERMINOLOGY.md):

- **Parts location field (established in episode 2):** Spare parts have a location field that means the same item type can be registered in several locations — the main warehouse and a specific service van as its own location. Source: Product Truth §9 (Maintenance) and Capability Index #75.
- **Booking information as the basis for preparation:** A booking brings customer, locations, dates and requirements/task information together in one place, which is what Micke uses when he sees in advance what an upcoming job requires. Source: Terminology — core entity map ("Booking: … requirements, charges/costs, documents and tracking").
- **Stock adjustment with a reason (move between locations):** The story's "move" of a part between the main warehouse and a service van is carried out in the source documentation as two reasoned balance adjustments — a decrease in one location, an increase in another — where each adjustment requires a stated reason and writes a traceable stock movement. Source: Product Truth §9 ("Stock adjustment requires a reason and writes a ledger movement").

**Explicit limitation (what is NOT claimed):** The story does not claim that there is a dedicated, named "Transfer" button specifically for Parts (the richer set with Transfer/Reserve/Release/Stocktake is documented for Stock Levels/Articles in the Commerce module, not for Parts). What is depicted — a part being booked out of one location and added to another, both with a stated reason — is instead based on the simpler, verified Parts adjustment function. The story also does not claim that the system automatically suggests which parts should be prepared for a job; it is Micke who actively reads the booking and acts on it.
