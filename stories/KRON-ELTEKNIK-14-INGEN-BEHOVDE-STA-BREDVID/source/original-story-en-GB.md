# NO ONE NEEDED TO STAND BESIDE HER
### A navichain story — Nadja, Yvonne and the first day that did not turn out as expected

**Persona:** Nadja Holm, 26, newly hired field technician, Kron Elteknik AB
**Co-lead:** Yvonne Kron, coordinator/dispatcher, Kron Elteknik AB
**Supporting character:** Micke Sandin, Daniel Ohls (cameo)
**Persona thread:** Episode 14 in the Kron Elteknik thread — resolves the teaser from "The Invoice That Was Only Written Once": a new technician gets started without anyone having to stand beside her and show everything from scratch
**Theme:** Onboarding new staff — from an expected, resource-intensive training period to an app that is understandable enough to manage on your own from day one
**Navichain's role in the story:** The mobile app's clear, step-by-step flow for assignment, arrival and evidence capture (established in earlier episodes) allows a completely new technician to carry out her first job independently, without an experienced colleague having to set aside their own working day to stand beside her

**Format:** 12 panels + cover concept, Swedish dialogue/captions in the original, warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin style (ligne claire), parchment/cream base palette with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold, hand-drawn captions, subtle print grain and patina — modern context, classic visual language
**Colour dramaturgy:** 🔴 Red (problem, panels 1–6) → 🟠 Orange (turning point, panel 7) → 🟢 Petroleum green (solution, panels 8–12)

**Logo rule (mandatory):** navichain's official logo is completely absent in panels 1–6. It is introduced discreetly from panel 7 onwards — as an environmental detail (the app header on the phone screen, a sticker on the service van), never centred or advertising-like, max. approx. 5% of the panel area. ## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-CAPABILITY-INDEX.md):

- **The mobile app's clear assignment flow (established in episode 1):** Assignment, acceptance, arrival and execution take place in a step-by-step flow in the mobile app, with photo/signature/automatic GPS/time on completion. Source: Capability Index #127–128; Product Truth §14 (Mobile App).
- **No separate login is required beyond a linked user account:** Mobile execution requires a linked user account, represented in the story by Nadja already having been given an account (linked to her profile) before she starts using the app. Source: Product Truth §17 ("Driver-only | Booking grid is read-only with History; mobile execution requires a linked user account").

**Explicit limitation (what is NOT claimed):** The story does not claim that Navichain contains a special "onboarding guide" or built-in training module for new employees — what is depicted is that the ordinary, already established assignment flow in the mobile app is clear enough for a new user to follow without external guidance. Nor does the story claim that all work tasks (for example, technical trade skills) are taught by the app — it specifically concerns how an assignment is received, carried out and documented in the system, not professional trade competence itself.
