# A Navichain story

## “The summer temp who was never allowed to drive — the qualification filter that saved the insurance cover”

**Character:** Örjan Berg, 52 — Transport Manager, Ösjöns Frakt & Bud AB (28 vehicles, freight and groupage transport in the Sundsvall region, a high ADR share during the summer season) **Supporting character:** Jimmy Lindqvist — newly hired seasonal temporary worker, supplied through a staffing agency **Supporting character:** Anette Holm — contact person, Nordixi Bemanning AB **Cameo (continuity):** Robban Ek — regular ADR driver, recurring series character (appears in panel 8 as the solution when he steps in at short notice) **Source:** blog.navichain.se/resurshantering — Pillar 4: Resource Management (the sections on qualification filtering during run planning and support for hired/temporary drivers)

**Chosen angle (new to the series):** The Susanne Wikström story (“90 days’ head start”) has already covered competence registers, YKB/ADR monitoring and SOP reading acknowledgement from an HR/quality and tender perspective with permanently employed staff. This story takes an unused angle from the same source article: **hired/temporary drivers** and **automatic qualification filtering during run planning** — seen through a transport manager’s operational everyday work during seasonal peaks, with **insurance risk** (not tenders or roadside inspections) as the dramatic edge. Örjan is the series’ first transport-manager persona whose core problem is staffing through external personnel, unlike Susanne’s HR/quality role and earlier roadside-inspection stories.

**Next story thread:** Ends with a teaser towards resource matching — how Navichain automatically suggests “the right driver, the right vehicle, the right load” during run planning (linked to upcoming blog content about resource matching).

## Visual style (standard for the whole series)

Warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin *ligne claire* aesthetic. Clean, even-weight black contour lines. Flat, even colour fields — no halftone shading, no heavy raster shading. Parchment/cream background. Palette: rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green, warm gold. Captions are rendered as handwritten/hand-lettered text — never digital sans-serif. Physical props (binders, papers, stamps, noticeboards) are prioritised over screenshots; retro CRT monitors are used where a screen is required. Characters should be warm, expressive and genuinely happy in the resolution panels. The surface should feel lightly grainy, with subtle paper texture, soft edge wear/foxing and slightly faded ink tones — like a well-thumbed 1950s magazine.

**Narrative structure:** 12 panels + cover. Colour dramaturgy: red (crisis, panels 1–3) → orange (turning point, panels 4–6) → petroleum green (solution, panels 7–12). The final panel contains a teaser for the next part. No IT support appears — Örjan navigates Navichain entirely on his own, underlining that the product is self-explanatory.

**Logo:** Completely absent from panels 1–6 (problem phase). Introduced discreetly from panel 7 as an environmental detail (screen header, mug, document corner) — never more than ~5% of the panel area, never centred or advertisement-like. Use Navichain’s official logo file (uploaded as a source file in the project) for every placement — do not invent a variant.

## Illustrator notes

- **Continuity:** Robban Ek should be drawn recognisably the same as in earlier stories (same build, hair colour, type of jacket) for series continuity.
- **Panels 1–6 props:** Bakelite telephone, paper sheets, crumpled certificate copies, whiteboard calendar — reinforce the contrast with the digital system introduced in panels 6–7.
- **Panel 7 detail:** The warning for Jimmy should feel like a discovery the system makes in time — not as blame directed at Jimmy himself. Jimmy never appears in the image as a “villain”; he is simply a person whose paperwork became out of date.
- **Panel 10:** The insurance manager should be drawn neutral and professional — no caricature.
- **Logo:** Use Navichain’s official logo file (source file in the project) for all placements in panels 7–12. No invented or stylised variant.
