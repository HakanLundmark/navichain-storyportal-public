# THE NOTE THAT WAS NEVER WRITTEN
### A Navichain story — Daniel, Yvonne and the promise that could not be forgotten

**Persona:** Daniel Ohls, 31, field technician, Kron Elteknik AB
**Co-protagonist:** Yvonne Kron, 44, coordinator/dispatcher, Kron Elteknik AB
**New supporting character:** Barbro Fält, property manager and recurring customer
**Persona thread:** Episode 3 in the Kron Elteknik thread — a direct continuation of "The Job No One Could Dispute" and "The Return Trip That Never Needed to Happen"
**Theme:** Follow-up in the field — promises made verbally at the door and then lost in the volume of work, instead of becoming a traceable task
**Navichain's role in the story:** The Tasks Board that captures follow-up needs as soon as they arise — with status, assignee and due date — so that nothing merely "exists in someone's head"

**Note on the previous teaser:** The previous episode teased "the customer's own booking through the portal without a phone call to Yvonne". That angle — the Customer Portal's self-service order creation with the "Requires Review" flag — turned out to have already been covered earlier in the series (the Anette Malm/Sara Lund episode). This episode therefore takes a genuinely unused angle instead: internal follow-up tasks that would otherwise be forgotten.

**Format:** 12 panels + cover concept, Swedish dialogue/captions, warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin style (ligne claire), parchment/cream base palette with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold, hand-drawn captions, subtle print grain and patina — modern context, classic visual language
**Colour dramaturgy:** 🔴 Red (problem, panels 1–6) → 🟠 Orange (turning point, panel 7) → 🟢 Petroleum green (solution, panels 8–12)

**Logo rule (mandatory):** Navichain's official logo is completely absent from panels 1–6. It is introduced discreetly from panel 7 onward — as an environmental detail (the app header on a mobile screen, a board on an office wall screen, a document stamp), never centred or advertising-like, maximum about 5% of the panel area. ## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-CAPABILITY-INDEX.md, NAVICHAIN-TERMINOLOGY.md):

- **Tasks Board:** A full configurable kanban board as well as a lighter quick-triage variant, with fields for title/description/tags/status/priority/type/one assignee/due date, checklist and comments. The columns are tenant-configurable. Source: Product Truth §12 (Workspace) and Capability Index #114.
- **Status change:** Users drag the status or edit it explicitly — there is no automatic movement of a task between columns without someone actively doing it (except for certain system-generated tasks; see the boundary below). Source: Product Truth §12.
- **No archive:** The Tasks Board has no archive — completed tasks remain visible rather than disappearing from the system. Source: Product Truth §18 (Status Models — "Task | Tenant-configured columns | User drag/edit or automatic creation; no archive").

**Explicit boundary (what is NOT claimed):** The story does not claim that the task in panel 8 is created automatically by the system — Daniel creates it himself, a user-initiated step. Product Truth documents that certain tasks *can* be created automatically, but only for failed inspections/compliance deviations (Capability Index #115), not for verbal customer requests. The story also does not claim that the board has a calendar function, recurring tasks or a full root cause/CAPA flow — according to Product Truth, these are either absent or only partially verified in the current interface.
