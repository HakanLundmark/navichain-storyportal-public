# A NAVICHAIN STORY

## "Six Months in Five Days"

### Berit and the Archive That Never Forgot

**Format:** 12-panel illustrated storyboard, brief for illustrator  
**Persona (main character):** Berit Holmgren, 44, Quality and Sustainability Manager at Holmgrens Frigo & Fjärrtransport AB (20 vehicles, food transport in Sweden and the Nordic region)  
**Product angle:** Automatic handling of tachograph data (HaaS telematics, Teltonika FMC650) — continuous archiving of driving-time data per driver, searchable history and export directly in the Swedish Transport Agency's format  
**Source:** demosite.navichain.se/plattformen/forarapp/

## VISUAL STYLE (applies to all panels)

Warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin *ligne claire* aesthetic: clean contour lines of even thickness, flat colour fields without halftones or heavy cross-hatching. Parchment/cream base with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold. Captions are hand-lettered, never digital sans-serif. Physical props (USB sticks in envelopes, binders, filing cabinets, stamps) are prioritised over screens — where a screen is unavoidable (Berit's search view in Navichain), it is drawn as a warm, rounded monitor in the series' established retro spirit. Light grainy offset-print texture with patina, soft edge wear and foxing. Characters are warm and expressive, genuinely happy and smiling in the resolution panels.

**Colour dramaturgy:** Panels 1–4 red; panels 5–6 orange/mustard yellow; panel 7 red → green; panels 8–12 petroleum green.

**Logo placement:** the Navichain logo is completely absent from panels 1–6; introduced discreetly from panel 7; maximum about 5% of panel area; use only the official Navichain logo.

## PANEL 1

**Image:** Berit's office. She holds up a letter with the Swedish Transport Agency letterhead, her face pale. Part of the text on the letter is visible: "...audit of driving and rest times for all vehicles, the last six months. Documentation must be submitted within five working days."  
**Caption:** "Six months. Twenty trucks. Five days to do it. Berit read the line twice more."

## PANEL 2

**Image:** An open filing cabinet full of small brown envelopes, each envelope handwritten with a registration number and a date — some envelopes are completely missing from the row.  
**Caption:** "Every driver was supposed to download their USB stick every week. Most did. Not all."

## PANEL 3

**Image:** Berit at an old computer, trying to read a damaged USB stick, an error box flashing. Beside her is a list of driver names where three are ticked as "week 14 missing".  
**Caption:** "One stick was corrupt. Two drivers couldn't remember whether they had downloaded at all."

## PANEL 4

**Image:** Berit with her head in her hands at the desk, a calendar with "5 days left" circled in red and an imagined consequence image.  
**Caption:** "Without complete documentation, the haulage company risks both fines and its operating licence being called into question."

## PANEL 5

**Image:** Robban Ek pokes his head through the door and mentions that his truck "does that automatically now" since the workshop installed a new unit.  
**Caption:** "'Automatically?' Berit had not even thought to ask."

## PANEL 6

**Image:** Berit at the computer, logging in to Navichain for the first time, with a simple search box visible: "Tachograph data — select period." Beside her is the HaaS telematics brochure.  
**Caption:** "If Robban's truck was already collecting it — what else had already been saved without anyone asking for it?"

## PANEL 7 — TRANSITION PANEL

**Image:** Red left/green right: the sparse filing cabinet versus an imagined digital timeline without gaps.  
**Caption:** "No gap. No forgotten download. The data had been collected every second, all the time."

## PANEL 8

**Image:** Berit's screen shows all 20 vehicles and six months of history.  
**Caption:** "Six months, twenty trucks — already there, already complete. She only had to ask for it."

## PANEL 9

**Image:** Berit selects a date range and clicks "Export to the Swedish Transport Agency."  
**Caption:** "The right format, the right period, one click. Nothing copied by hand."

## PANEL 10

**Image:** Berit hands a neatly bundled document to an auditor two days before the deadline.  
**Caption:** "Two days left out of five. The auditor found nothing to remark on."

## PANEL 11

**Image:** A formal approval letter from the Swedish Transport Agency lies open on Berit's desk. Robban is visible in the background.  
**Caption:** "No fine. No withdrawn licence. Just a letter with the one word she had been waiting for: approved."

## PANEL 12

**Image:** Berit's office, late afternoon. She stands beside the old filing cabinet, now empty and switched out of use, with the sign "Can be discarded" and a coffee mug with the Navichain logo.  
**Caption:** "The cabinet with the brown envelopes was allowed to retire. The data never forgets — so Berit no longer has to do it for it."

**Teaser:** *"Next story: what happens when a customer wants to change their booking five minutes before pickup?"*

## ILLUSTRATOR NOTES

Robban Ek: dark cap, blue jacket. Berit Holmgren: 44 years old, practical office clothing, stressed posture at the beginning that softens towards the end. Use only the official Navichain logo. Screens in the established retro-monitor style. The focus is an authority audit of historical data and the contrast between a thinning manual archive and an unbroken digital history.

## ACCEPTED PREFLIGHT REVISIONS IN THIS CHAT

The user replied: **"go with your recommendation"**. The following corrections were then accepted as the production basis:

- The authority audit period itself changes from six months to two months; six months instead describes the available history.
- Panel 1: "...audit of driving and rest times for the period 1 June–31 July. The documentation must be submitted within five working days." Caption: "Two months were to be audited. Twenty trucks. Five days to do it. Berit read the line twice more."
- Panel 5: the telematics equipment is stated to have been installed across the whole fleet earlier, not only in Robban's truck.
- Panel 7: "No forgotten download. No lost stick. The tachograph files had been retrieved automatically and archived continuously."
- Panel 8: "Six months of history, twenty trucks — already archived. The audit period was only a search away."
- Panel 9: audit period 2026-06-01–2026-07-31; "Export audit documentation"; vehicle-unit files and driver-card files as raw data.
- Panel 10: submission through the Swedish Transport Agency's e-service and a receipt confirmation instead of physical handover to an auditor.
- Panel 11: the result is phrased as no infringements requiring action being found, rather than as an invented exact authority form.
- Panel 12: "no manual handling" refers to manual downloading and archiving of digital tachograph files, not all compliance administration.

This document preserves both the directly submitted brief and the explicitly accepted corrections without recreating missing image material.
