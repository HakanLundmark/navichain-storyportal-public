# Storyboard: "Out of the black box" — Torbjörn takes back control of logistics

**Part of:** navichain story (standalone story, same universe as Maria/Johan/Elin/Fredrik/Åsa/Stefan et al.)  
**Source:** https://blog.navichain.se/oberoende/ ("The great logistics insourcing: Why European cargo owners are taking back control")  
**Protagonist:** Torbjörn Askeland, 47 — Head of Logistics, Nordiska Hemvaror AB (growing e-commerce company for home furnishings, Uppsala, daily pallet and parcel shipments to stores and private customers across the Nordic region)  
**Supporting character:** Voice on the phone/on the invoice — contact person at the fictional 3PL company "NordCargo Solutions" (never shown, only represented by a telephone receiver/logo on invoice paper)  
**Angle:** This is the series' first story about **logistics insourcing** — a cargo owner moving from fully outsourcing its logistics to a 3PL operator, to owning the decision layer itself (booking, follow-up, data) through its own TMS. It differs clearly from Fredrik's story (customer portal for booking with several navichain-connected hauliers) and Petra's/Sofia's stories (data sovereignty against the CLOUD Act/FISA 702) by focusing on **economic and operational control in relation to a 3PL intermediary**, not government access.

**Format:** 12-panel illustrator brief + cover concept  
**Colour dramaturgy:** 🔴 Red (panels 1–6, the problem: the black box at the 3PL) → 🟠 Orange (panel 7, the turning point: the decision to insource) → 🟢 Petroleum green (panels 8–12, the solution: own control tower with navichain)

---

## Visual style (standard instruction — applies to all panels)

Warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin *ligne claire* aesthetic: clean, even black contour lines, flat/even colour areas (no heavy halftone shading or cross-hatching), parchment/cream-coloured base background with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold. Captions hand-drawn/handwritten, never digital sans-serif. Physical props (documents, stamps, seals, signs, cork noticeboards, clipboards) are prioritised over UI screenshots where possible; retro CRT screens where a screen is nevertheless needed. The characters should be warm, expressive and genuinely happy in the resolution panels. Texture: lightly grainy offset-print feel with aged patina — subtle paper texture, soft edge wear/foxing, muted/slightly faded tonal values, like a well-thumbed 1950s magazine.

**Logo placement (standard rule):** navichain's official logo (uploaded as a source file in the project — use that file, invent no variant of your own) is completely absent in panels 1–6. It is introduced discreetly from panel 7 and gradually integrated into panels 8–12 — on a coffee mug, folder layout, a small screen-corner icon, a vehicle sticker — never more than about 5% of the panel area, never centred or advertising-like.

**Governing principle:** No IT support appears in any panel. Torbjörn and his colleagues navigate navichain entirely on their own — the platform is self-explanatory.

### Panel 1

**Image:** Torbjörn's office at Nordiska Hemvaror, Monday morning. He is sitting with a paper invoice from NordCargo Solutions in his hand, eyebrows raised. Several lines with cryptic surcharges are underlined in red pen: "fuel surcharge", "time booking surcharge", "handling fee". A calculator lies beside it.  
**Text:** "The invoice is as thick as a telephone directory. Not a single line explains what the freight actually cost to run."  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — hidden pricing

### Panel 2

**Image:** Torbjörn on the phone, with an on-hold-music expression, eyes looking up at the ceiling. Three months of invoices are stacked on the desk. A notepad with question marks and crossed-out amounts.  
**Text:** "He calls to ask why the bill went up again. Twenty minutes in the queue — then an answer he cannot verify."  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — no visibility

### Panel 3

**Image:** Close-up of Torbjörn's desk: three invoices side by side, with a ruler and red pen marking percentages in the margins — 14%, 21%, 26%. A yellowed note with "where does the money go?" written by hand.  
**Text:** "Calculated over a quarter: between one in seven and one in four kronor disappears into the intermediary's mark-up."  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — the hidden mark-up

### Panel 4

**Image:** The phone rings again — this time it is a store manager on the other end, visibly upset (shown through Torbjörn's facial expression and a speech bubble with an exclamation mark). Torbjörn frantically leafs through a binder labelled "DELIVERIES" without finding a tracking number that matches.  
**Text:** "A customer is waiting for a sofa delivery that should have arrived yesterday. Torbjörn has no answer — only a binder that cannot keep up."  
**Status:** 🔴 Red — customer trust is eroded

### Panel 5

**Image:** Torbjörn alone at his desk in the evening, the office empty outside the window. He leans back with his hands clasped behind his neck and looks at the pile of invoices and the silent binder. An expression of determination, not resignation.  
**Text:** "'We own the goods. We own the customer relationship. But we do not own a single data flow showing how they actually get there.'"  
**Status:** 🔴→🟠 Turning point — the realisation

### Panel 6

**Image:** Torbjörn bent over the desk with a pen, drawing a simple sketch on a notepad: a box labelled "NordCargo" with a cross through it, an arrow to a new box he writes himself: "OWN TMS + DIRECT CONTRACTS". Beside it lies a printout he ordered from the 3PL portal — a list of routes and volumes.  
**Text:** "He orders his own history — routes, volumes, cost per zone. That data is his. He intends to stop lending it out."  
**Status:** 🟠 Orange — the plan takes shape

### Panel 7

**Image:** Torbjörn's office, now with a simple clipboard on the wall: "STEP 1: MAP. STEP 2: NEGOTIATE. STEP 3: IMPLEMENT." He shakes hands with two freight forwarders (shown through two extended sleeves/hands in the frame, faces partly cropped to keep the focus on Torbjörn) in front of a map with two marked routes. A computer with a discreet navichain logo in the corner of the screen is visible for the first time on the desk.  
**Text:** "Two direct contracts, signed on his own using his volume history as the basis. Next step: a system that holds everything together."  
**Status:** 🟠 Orange — direct contracts are signed, navichain is introduced

### Panel 8

**Image:** Torbjörn at the computer, relaxed posture, a coffee cup with the discreet navichain logo next to the keyboard. He drags and drops an email with an order confirmation straight into navichain's inbox — no manual typing. A small tick icon appears automatically.  
**Text:** "Orders come in by email as before — but now they are read on their own. No manual entry, no waiting for support."  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — self-running order intake

### Panel 9

**Image:** A retro CRT screen on Torbjörn's desk shows a simple, clear overview: a map of Sweden with dots for ongoing deliveries, a small table with "Status: Green", "Jobs: 12 active". Physical props around the screen: a binder labelled "CONTRACTS — DIRECT", a clipboard with ticked-off routes.  
**Text:** "For the first time he sees everything at once — where every parcel is, with which freight forwarder, in real time."  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — full overview, own control tower

### Panel 10

**Image:** The phone rings — the same store manager as in panel 4. Torbjörn answers calmly, at the same time pointing with his pen at the screen where the delivery is clearly marked "Estimated arrival 14:20". The store manager's tone (shown through a relieved speech bubble) goes from concern to relief.  
**Text:** "The same question as last time. This time he has the answer before she has time to ask it."  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — proactive customer service

### Panel 11

**Image:** Torbjörn and a colleague (controller/finance manager) review together a physical printed cost sheet beside the screen — cost per zone and freight forwarder, clearly compared with the old 3PL invoices. A stamp: "SWEDISH SERVERS — GDPR" is discreetly visible in the corner of the screen.  
**Text:** "The cost is now there in black and white, zone by zone. No guessing game — and the data stays with them."  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — cost control and data ownership

### Panel 12

**Image:** Torbjörn and the colleague clink coffee mugs (the navichain logo discreetly visible on both mugs) in front of the screen, evening sun through the office window. On the noticeboard behind them hangs a small note: "Next: when the vehicles themselves tell us where they are." A checklist on the desk is completely ticked off: "Mapped ✓ Negotiated ✓ Implemented ✓ Saved 22%".  
**Text:** "No intermediary between him and the truth any more. Just his data, his contracts — and his control."  
**Status:** 🟢 Green — result & teaser for the next story

## Cover concept

**Subject:** Torbjörn in the foreground, a heavy 3PL invoice in one hand about to be put aside, the other hand resting lightly on an open laptop where a simple, clean overview is visible. In the background: a blurred, grey-toned "black box" with a padlock breaking open and releasing light — a symbolic, non-photorealistic illustration rather than a literal scene. navichain's official logo discreetly visible in the corner of the computer screen.  
**Title:** OUT OF THE BLACK BOX  
**Subtitle:** Torbjörn & the road to his own control tower  
**Kicker:** "Whoever owns the data owns the truth about their own delivery."

---

**Continuity with the series:** Shares the visual universe and colour dramaturgy with other navichain stories. Torbjörn is the series' first dedicated **cargo-owner/head-of-logistics perspective on insourcing from a 3PL** — distinct from Fredrik's customer-portal angle (booking with several connected hauliers) and Petra's/Sofia's data-sovereignty angle (protection against foreign government access) by being about leaving a 3PL intermediary completely and bringing both contracts and data in-house. No IT support appears. navichain's official logo is used according to the applicable placement rules (absent in panels 1–6, discreet props from panel 7). Teaser for the next story: own vehicle telematics (HaaS) — a track not yet used in the series.
