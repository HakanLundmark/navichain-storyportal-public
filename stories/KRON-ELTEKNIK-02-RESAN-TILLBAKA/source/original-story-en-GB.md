## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-CAPABILITY-INDEX.md, NAVICHAIN-TERMINOLOGY.md):

- **Parts (spare-parts register):** A maintenance catalogue/stock register separate from commercial Articles, with category, supplier, stock level, cost and **location** as separate fields. Source: Product Truth §9 (Maintenance) and Capability Index #75.
- **Stock adjustment with reason:** Adjusting a stock level requires a stated reason and writes a ledger movement — which in the story corresponds to Daniel's withdrawal of the contactor being recorded with a reason rather than simply disappearing without a trace. Source: Product Truth §9.
- **Draft PO reorder:** "Order" creates a draft purchase order to replenish to a set minimum level before the stock runs out completely. Source: Product Truth §9 ("Order creates a draft PO to replenish the minimum").
- **Location as its own field:** Representing a service van as its own stock location next to the main warehouse is based on Parts having a location field in the base register — it does not portray a separate "vehicle stock module", but a use of the location field that already exists.

**Explicit limitation (what is NOT claimed):** The story does not claim that the stock level updates automatically without human registration, that the order is sent to the supplier without approval (it is a draft awaiting the user's decision), or that the system physically detects what is in a van without someone registering it. What is portrayed is what is documented: a location-linked stock level, a reasoned adjustment and a proposed draft order at a low level.
