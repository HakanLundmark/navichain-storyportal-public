# ONE NAME FOR ALL THE BUILDINGS
### A navichain story — Cornelia, Birgitta and the family of buildings that finally fit together

**Persona:** Cornelia Strand, local coordinator, Kron Elteknik AB (the new location)
**New supporting character:** Birgitta Orre, Head of Property Management at Fastighetsgruppen Örnen AB
**Supporting character:** Yvonne Kron (cameo)
**Persona thread:** Episode 16 in the Kron Elteknik thread — after "Two offices, one list that did not know who was who"
**Theme:** Large customers with many addresses — from several disconnected, almost identical customer records to a clear family of buildings under the same main customer
**Navichain's role in the story:** An organization can have a parent and linked child organizations, so that several buildings belonging to the same large customer appear as what they actually are — one relationship, not several separate strangers

**Note on the previous teaser:** The originally announced angle (local customer agreements growing beyond what anyone can keep in their head) was found to be too close to an angle already used in the series — pricing consistency at owner level (the Björklunda thread, Torbjörn's episode). This episode therefore uses a related but genuinely different, unused mechanism instead: an organization hierarchy for a large customer with multiple addresses.

**Format:** 12 panels + cover concept, Swedish dialogue/captions, warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin style (ligne claire), parchment/cream base palette with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold, hand-drawn captions, subtle print grain and patina — modern context, classic visual language
**Colour dramaturgy:** 🔴 Red (problem, panels 1–6) → 🟠 Orange (turning point, panel 7) → 🟢 Petroleum green (solution, panels 8–12)

**Logo rule (mandatory):** navichain's official logo is completely absent from panels 1–6. It is introduced discreetly from panel 7 onwards — as an environmental detail (the app header on a phone/computer screen, a letterhead), never centred or advertisement-like, max. approx. 5% of the panel area. The illustrator must use the uploaded source file for navichain's official logo.

## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-TERMINOLOGY.md, NAVICHAIN-CAPABILITY-INDEX.md):

- **Parent organization and child organizations:** An organization record can have a parent that groups several child organizations/subsidiaries. Source: Terminology — "Parent organization | Organization hierarchy used to group subsidiaries/child organizations"; Product Truth §9 (Partners & Locations) — "Organizations hold unique name, registration/VAT/EORI/external number, contact, parent, terms, currency, language..."
- **Consolidated relationship view:** An organization's detail view shows bookings, runs, subsidiaries, portal users, pricing/surcharges and addresses together. Source: Product Truth §9 — "Details show bookings, runs, subsidiaries, portal users, pricing/surcharges and addresses."

**Explicit boundary (what is NOT claimed):** The story does not claim that the system automatically detects or suggests that two customer records actually belong together — Cornelia and Yvonne actively identify and link them. Nor does it claim that terms/prices are automatically inherited or applied identically from the parent to all child organizations; the source documentation describes the hierarchy and the consolidated view, not automatic pricing-rule inheritance.
