### A navichain story — Siv, Daniel and the answer that was already there

**Persona:** Siv Ekwall, 51, finance assistant, Kron Elteknik AB  
**Co-protagonist:** Daniel Ohls, 31, field technician, Kron Elteknik AB (mentioned/cameo)

## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-CAPABILITY-INDEX.md):

- **The booking as the central information carrier (established in episode 1):** A booking gathers the customer, locations, dates, job information, documents and tracking in one place — including the photos/proof documentation recorded when the job is completed. Source: Terminology — core entity map.
- **Mobile proof as part of the booking documentation:** Photos and signatures recorded when an assignment is completed are linked to the booking and thereby become searchable/visible to whoever later opens the same booking, regardless of whether it is the same person who performed the job. Source: Capability Index #128 ("Mobile proof photos/signature plus automatic GPS/time").
- **Role-based access, not person-bound knowledge:** The fact that an employee other than the original technician can open and read a booking is based on the system’s ordinary permission model (roles/claims), not on a special "knowledge sharing" function. Source: Product Truth §17 (Roles & Permissions).

**Explicit limitation (what is NOT claimed):** The story does not claim that the system automatically identifies or suggests which booking answers an incoming customer question — Siv looks up the correct booking herself. Nor does it claim that every technical detail of a job is always captured in a photo; in this story, the particular information being requested happens to be clearly visible in the documentation already collected.
