## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-TERMINOLOGY.md, NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md):

- **Department with inherited permissions:** A department is a staff group whose permissions are inherited through a parent chain. Source: Terminology — "Department | Staff group with parent-chain permission inheritance"; Product Truth §13 (Administration) — "Department parent-chain permission inheritance | Administration/Access | CONFIGURATION-DEPENDENT | VERIFIED."
- **Roles and claims as the basis for what is visible:** What a user can see and manage is controlled by roles/claims linked to the account, which is the general mechanism that the departmental structure builds on. Source: Product Truth §17 (Roles & Permissions).

**Explicit boundary (what is NOT being claimed):** The story does not claim that the departmental structure is a special "multi-location feature" built specifically for companies with multiple offices — it is the general department/permission mechanism being used here to model two locations. Nor does it claim that the system automatically detects or warns about geographical distance between a job and a technician's location; the separation happens through which bookings appear at all in each department's view, not through a distance calculation.
