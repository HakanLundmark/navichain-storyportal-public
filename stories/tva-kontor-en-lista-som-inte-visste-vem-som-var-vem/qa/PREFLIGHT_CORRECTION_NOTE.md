# Preflight correction note

The preflight in this chat concluded that the story's core market problem is plausible and relevant, but several Product Truth claims were too strong.

Supported: Departments exist and permission unions inherit from ancestors in the department parent chain.

Not verified by the available Product Truth: that department membership itself scopes Booking/Staff rows so a local user automatically sees only that department's bookings, or that an ancestor role automatically gets a combined child-department booking view.

Therefore panel claims 2–6 and especially 9–11 require revision before Story Lock. The proposed meaning-preserving correction was to frame the payoff as explicit organizational structure and inherited permissions, not verified booking-row segregation.
