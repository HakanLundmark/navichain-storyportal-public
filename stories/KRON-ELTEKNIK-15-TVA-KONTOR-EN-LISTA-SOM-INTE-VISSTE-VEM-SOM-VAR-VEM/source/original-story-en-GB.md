# TWO OFFICES, ONE LIST THAT DIDN'T KNOW WHO WAS WHO
### A navichain story — Yvonne, Cornelia and the boundary that was finally drawn

**Persona:** Yvonne Kron, coordinator/dispatcher, Kron Elteknik AB (main location)
**New supporting character:** Cornelia Strand, 38, new local coordinator for Kron Elteknik's newly opened second location
**Supporting character:** Rasmus Öberg (cameo), Micke Sandin
**Persona thread:** Episode 15 in the Kron Elteknik thread — resolves the teaser from "Ingen behövde stå bredvid": a second operating location
**Theme:** Growth across multiple locations — from a single shared list where no one knows what is "their own" to a clear department structure where each location sees its own, while the owner still sees everything
**Navichain's role in the story:** A department structure with inherited permissions (parent-chain) lets each location work in its own bounded view — without building two separate systems or losing the combined overview at owner level

**Format:** 12 panels + cover concept, Swedish dialogue/captions in the Narrative Master, warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin-style ligne claire, parchment/cream base palette with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold, hand-drawn captions, subtle print grain and patina — modern context, classic visual language
**Colour dramaturgy:** 🔴 Red (problem, panels 1–6) → 🟠 Orange (turning point, panel 7) → 🟢 Petroleum green (solution, panels 8–12)

**Logo rule (mandatory):** navichain's official logo is completely absent in panels 1–6. It is introduced discreetly from panel 7 onwards — as an environmental detail (app header on a mobile/computer screen, a sign at the new office), never centred or advertising-like, max. about 5% of the panel area. ## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-TERMINOLOGY.md, NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md):

- **Department with inherited permissions:** A department is a staff group whose permissions are inherited through a parent chain (parent-chain). Source: Terminology — "Department | Staff group with parent-chain permission inheritance"; Product Truth §13 (Administration) — "Department parent-chain permission inheritance | Administration/Access | CONFIGURATION-DEPENDENT | VERIFIED."
- **Roles and claims as the basis for what is visible:** What a user can see and manage is controlled by roles/claims linked to the account, which is the general mechanism on which the department structure builds. Source: Product Truth §17 (Roles & Permissions).

**Explicit limitation (what is NOT claimed):** The story does not claim that the department structure is a special "multi-location feature" built specifically for companies with several offices — it is the general department/permission mechanism used here to model two locations. Nor does it claim that the system automatically detects or warns about geographic distance between a job and a technician's location; the separation happens through which bookings are visible at all in each department's view, not through a distance calculation.
