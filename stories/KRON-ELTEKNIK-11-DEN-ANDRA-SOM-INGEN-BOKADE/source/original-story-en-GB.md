## Product Truth grounding and boundaries

- Runs support multiple drivers.
- When a Run becomes Confirmed, an assignment notification may be sent to assigned drivers through configured channels; the exact channel depends on the tenant/configuration.
- The story does not claim that Navichain automatically decides that a job requires two people.
- The story does not claim that an assignment notification is guaranteed to be read in time.
- The safe formulation is that both are assigned to the same Run/job and have the same operational job in front of them, not that every notification is word-for-word identical across all channels.
