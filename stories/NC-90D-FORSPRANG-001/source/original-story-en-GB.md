## Preflight corrections included in the locked master

1. The hard-coded date `2026-04-14` was removed because it could not correspond to “six days ago” in August 2026. Panel 3 instead uses `R. EK — ADR — EXPIRED?`; panel 8 retains the discovery **EXPIRED 6 DAYS AGO**.
2. The Bakelite telephone, CRT screens and other actual 1950s technology were replaced with modern phones, a laptop and a present-day office environment. The classic feel belongs to the comic-album style, not to a historical story world.
3. The ISO wording was softened: timestamped acknowledgements and the competence register give Susanne traceable audit evidence, without claiming that ISO 9001 specifically requires Navichain's digital acknowledgement format.
