# A NAVICHAIN STORY

## "No one called — yet he knew"

### Gunilla, Robban and the communication that never disrupts the road ahead

**Format:** 12-panel illustrated storyboard, brief for illustrator **Persona (main character):** Gunilla Åhman, 51, traffic manager at Åhmans Åkeri & Distribution AB (14 vehicles, regional distribution in central Sweden) **Recurring series character:** Robban Ek, driver — the same Robban who has appeared in previous navichain stories **Product angle:** Driver App + HaaS telematics (Teltonika FMC650 — live GPS via SignalR) — communication with the traffic manager in the app and a push notification when rerouted, instead of phone calls to a driver who is driving **Source:** demosite.navichain.se/plattformen/forarapp/

---

## VISUAL STYLE (applies to all panels)

Warm 1950s aesthetic in Hergé/Tintin *ligne claire*: clean contour lines of even thickness, flat colour fields without halftones or heavy hatching. Parchment/cream background with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold. Captions hand-lettered, never digital sans-serif. Physical props (telephone receivers, notepads, clipboards, coffee mugs) are prioritised over screen views — where a screen is unavoidable (the traffic manager's fleet view, the Driver App on the dashboard), it is drawn as a warm, rounded monitor/touchpad in the series' established retro spirit, not a sharp modern UI mock-up. Light grainy offset-print texture with patina, soft edge wear and foxing. The characters are warm and expressive, genuinely happy and smiling in the resolution panels.

**Colour dramaturgy:**

- Panels 1–4: Red — problem/crisis
- Panels 5–6: Orange/mustard yellow — turning point
- Panel 7: Transition panel, red → green
- Panels 8–12: Petroleum green — solution

**Logo placement (the navichain logo):**

- Always called "the navichain logo" — never an "N mark" or a made-up symbol
- Completely absent in panels 1–6
- Introduced discreetly from panel 7: app header, coffee mug, edge of dashboard screen, document watermark
- Max \~5% of the panel area, never centred or promotional
- **Illustrator's note:** Use the official navichain logo (file supplied by Håkan) in all specified places — no reinterpretation or placeholder symbol

## ILLUSTRATOR NOTES

- **Continuity character:** Robban Ek is drawn consistently with the same appearance as in previous navichain stories (dark cap, blue jacket) for recognition across the series.
- **New main character:** Gunilla Åhman — woman, 51, practical office clothing, a warm but stressed expression in the opening panels that softens towards the end.
- **Logo file:** Use only the official navichain logo (supplied by Håkan) in all specified places.
- **Screens:** Keep all screen views (panels 7–10, 12) in the series' established retro-monitor style.
- **Angle note:** This story is separate from earlier episodes about driving-time alerts (Karin/Robban, "Five minutes to the border") and driving-time planning (Jukka/Erik). The focus here is specifically on **traffic-manager communication and rerouting without calling a driver who is driving** — a safety angle made possible by the HaaS telematics' continuous live position, linked to the Driver App's push notifications.
- **Safety detail to preserve throughout:** Robban must never be shown holding the phone or looking at the screen while the vehicle is moving — only when stationary (red light, parked vehicle). This is a deliberate part of the story's point and should not be compromised for dramatic effect.
