# — A Navichain story —

# NO SURPRISE AT THE BARRIER

### Nils and the passages that are never forgotten

**Angle:** The fifteenth episode in the series about connected workflows ties route planning and finance together — two of the series’ earlier threads (Nils’s weather episode, Åsa’s invoicing cycle) — around a problem that is small each time but adds up month after month: bridges, ferries and road tolls that are forgotten until the bill has already arrived.

**Source:** Navichain — Runs/Passages (verified: passage types include ferry, tunnel, bridge, vignette, road toll, low-emission zone, congestion charge and other, with operator, reference, date/country/cost/files. Route calculation detects ferry legs and suggests required toll/vignette rows — the dispatcher confirms the reference, time and cost themselves. No automatic, permanent insertion; the suggestion must be confirmed by a person.)

Nils previously planned the way through a storm. This time his task is about something less dramatic, but just as easy to miss: remembering the small costs that belong to a route before they turn up as an unpleasant surprise in the next month’s accounts.

## Cover concept

Nils at his desk, with a map on the screen where a planned route crosses a bridge. A small, discreet prompt box has appeared beside the bridge symbol: “Road toll detected — enter reference and cost.” He reaches towards the keyboard, already about to fill it in. Title lettering in the same rust-red/petroleum-green combination as the other covers in the series.

---

## PANEL 1 — The route across the bridge

**Visual:** Nils plans a new route that crosses a tolled bridge between two countries. He is focused on time and distance and is not thinking at all about what the passage will actually cost. **Text:** “Distance, time, mileage. The bridge is only a line on the map — not a cost yet.” **Status:** 🔴 Red — passage planned, cost invisible

## PANEL 2 — The receipt in the glove compartment

**Visual:** Robban Ek pays at a road barrier and puts the receipt in the glove compartment among other slips — there is no central place where it is recorded or linked to the right job. **Text:** “Another receipt, among other receipts. No one yet knows which job it belongs to.” **Status:** 🔴 Red — a cost without a home

## PANEL 3 — Åsa’s unexplained line

**Visual:** Weeks later, Åsa (from an earlier episode) sits with a cost report where one item — a bridge toll — cannot be linked to any specific booking. She sighs; she recognises the pattern. **Text:** “A cost with no connection. Again. The same thing, almost every month.” **Status:** 🔴 Red — the same forgetfulness, recurring

## PANEL 4 — The pattern Nils had not seen until now

**Visual:** Åsa shows Nils the summary: bridge tolls, ferry tickets and road tolls appear as loose costs, month after month, never linked to the right run in advance. **Text:** “He had never seen it as a pattern until she laid out the figures, together.” **Status:** 🔴 Red — a recurring problem, finally visible for what it is

## PANEL 5 — The turning point: the suggestion that appears by itself

**Visual:** Nils calculates the route for the next job over the same type of bridge. This time the route calculation automatically detects the passage and suggests a toll row — ready to be filled in, not already completed. **Text:** “The system sees the bridge. It does not fill in the cost itself — but it never lets him forget it.” **Status:** 🟠 Orange — passage detected, ready to confirm

## PANEL 6 — Nils fills it in, deliberately

**Visual:** Nils enters the reference number, expected time and cost for the passage directly on the run, before Robban has even loaded the truck. A deliberate decision, not an automatic guess. **Text:** “He enters it himself. But this time, before it has happened — not afterwards.” **Status:** 🟠 Orange — documented in advance, by a person

## PANEL 7 — The passage, linked to the right run

**Visual:** The run now clearly shows a passage row with bridge, reference and expected cost — visible to everyone who opens the same run, not hidden in a glove compartment. A discreet sticker with Navichain’s official logo is visible in the corner of the run view. **Text:** “The same cost. But now it lives where the job lives — not in a glove compartment.” **Status:** 🟢 Green — the cost belongs in the right place

## PANEL 8 — Robban drives through without thinking about it

**Visual:** Robban crosses the bridge exactly as planned and pays the expected toll — no surprise, no improvisation. He puts the receipt in a pocket knowing exactly where it belongs. **Text:** “No surprise at the barrier. Just exactly what he already knew would happen.” **Status:** 🟢 Green — passage completed as planned

## PANEL 9 — Åsa finds it immediately

**Visual:** Åsa opens the same cost report a month later — the bridge toll is already linked to the correct booking, with no loose line to chase. **Text:** “No loose line this month. The cost was already where it belonged.” **Status:** 🟢 Green — reconciliation no longer requires a hunt

## PANEL 10 — The next route, the same habit

**Visual:** Nils plans a completely different route, this time with a ferry leg. He fills in the passage as naturally as the rest of the planning — no longer an exception he sometimes forgets, just part of the routine. **Text:** “No longer something he remembers sometimes. Just part of how a route is planned now.” **Status:** 🟢 Green — a new habit, not a special case

## PANEL 11 — The list grows, in order

**Visual:** A number of runs are shown in a list, each with its passages clearly visible beside it — bridges, ferries and road tolls gathered and readable instead of scattered receipts. **Text:** “Bridges, ferries, road tolls — gathered where they actually belong.” **Status:** 🟢 Green — the costs gathered, not scattered

## PANEL 12 — Nils and Åsa, a calm afternoon

**Visual:** Nils and Åsa meet briefly in the corridor, she holding a coffee mug with Navichain’s official logo, while he nods towards the screen where the bridge map is still visible. Neither of them has anything to worry about this month. **Text:** “The bridge was always there. Now the cost is too — all the way from planning to accounting.” **Status:** 🟢 Green — the chain complete, from forgotten line to part of the routine

**Post-it on Nils’s desk (teaser):** *“Next: the quote that becomes a booking by itself, as soon as the customer says yes.”*

## Continuity note

The fifteenth episode in the series about connected workflows at Björklunda Åkeri AB, and the episode that most clearly ties together the areas of responsibility of two earlier protagonists: Nils’s planning and Åsa’s finance meet around the same previously invisible type of cost. Nils Åkerlund takes the lead for the second time in the series, and Åsa Renberg returns in a supporting role that shows how a small habit at the planning stage saves an entire reconciliation exercise at the other end of the chain.

**Next in the series:** The quote that becomes a booking by itself — how a quote confirmed by the customer automatically turns into a real booking, without anyone having to enter the same information again. An angle not yet used in the series.
