# THE MAN WHO WAS SUPPOSED TO BE IN TWO PLACES
### A navichain story — Yvonne, Rasmus and the clash that never happened again

**Persona:** Yvonne Kron, 44, coordinator/dispatcher, Kron Elteknik AB
**Co-lead:** Rasmus Öberg, 28, field technician, Kron Elteknik AB (new recurring character)
**Supporting character:** Daniel Ohls (cameo), plus an affected customer, Ingvar Malmros
**Persona thread:** Episode 4 in the Kron Elteknik arc — after "The Job No One Could Dispute", "The Return Trip That Never Needed to Happen" and "The Note That Was Never Written"
**Theme:** Scheduling several technicians at the same time — from a dispatcher who keeps everyone's days in her head to a system that warns before a clash occurs
**Navichain's role in the story:** Resource assignment during booking clearly shows whether a technician is already occupied at the selected time — so a double booking is caught before it becomes a missed customer visit, not afterwards

**Note on the previous teaser:** The previous episode teased exactly this angle — "nine technicians, nine different days" — and that is exactly what this episode resolves.

## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-WORKFLOWS.md, NAVICHAIN-CAPABILITY-INDEX.md):

- **Resource assignment with "busy badge":** When assigning a resource (a driver in the base documentation) to a job, the assignment module shows candidates' capacity/compliance as well as clear "busy" markers for already booked times. Source: NAVICHAIN-WORKFLOWS.md §5 ("Resource modal displays candidate capacity/compliance and busy badges") and the Product Truth Runs section ("Assign"). In this story, Kron Elteknik's field technicians are represented by the same resource-assignment function that applies to drivers in the base documentation.
- **"Fits first" sorting:** Unsuitable/busy candidates are not hidden; instead they are sorted lower in the list — which in the story is shown by the free technician being easy to find without Rasmus's name disappearing completely. Source: the same section.
- **Filtering by technician/driver:** Bookings and jobs can be filtered by driver/resource, which in the story corresponds to each technician's own day becoming visible and coherent. Source: Product Truth §22 (Search, Filters & Bulk Operations) and Runs-grid filters (driver/status/date range).

**Explicit limitation (what is NOT claimed):** The story does not claim that the system automatically chooses the right technician or completely prevents a double booking — the marker is decision support that Yvonne herself acts on, not a lock that makes the choice for her. Nor does it claim that all nine technicians' real-time positions are shown on a map in this flow; what is depicted is the assignment view's busy marker and the filtered overview of booked jobs by technician.
