# THE MAN WHO WAS SUPPOSED TO BE IN TWO PLACES
### A Navichain story — Yvonne, Rasmus and the clash that never happened again

**Persona:** Yvonne Kron, 44, coordinator/dispatcher, Kron Elteknik AB  
**Co-lead:** Rasmus Öberg, 28, field technician, Kron Elteknik AB (new recurring character)  
**Supporting character:** Daniel Ohls (cameo), plus an affected customer, Ingvar Malmros  
**Persona thread:** Episode 4 in the Kron Elteknik thread — after "The Job No One Could Dispute", "The Return Trip That Never Needed to Happen" and "The Note That Was Never Written"  
**Theme:** Scheduling several technicians at the same time — from a dispatcher who keeps everyone's days in her head to a system that warns before a clash occurs  
**Navichain's role in the story:** Resource assignment during booking clearly shows whether a technician is already busy at the selected time — so that a double booking is caught before it becomes a missed customer visit, not afterwards

**Note about the previous teaser:** The previous episode teased exactly this angle — "nine technicians, nine different days" — and that is exactly what this episode solves.

**Format:** 12 panels + cover concept, Swedish dialogue/captions in the master, warm 1950s Hergé/Tintin-style ligne claire, parchment/cream base palette with rust red, mustard yellow, petroleum green and warm gold, hand-drawn captions, discreet print grain and patina — modern context, classic visual language  
**Colour dramaturgy:** 🔴 Red (problem, panels 1–6) → 🟠 Orange (turning point, panel 7) → 🟢 Petroleum green (solution, panels 8–12)

**Logo rule (mandatory):** Navichain's official logo is completely absent from panels 1–6. It is introduced discreetly from panel 7 onwards — as an environmental detail (the app header on a mobile screen, a decal on the service van), never centred or advertising-like, max about 5% of the panel area. ## Product terminology and grounding

Grounded in verified Navichain documentation (NAVICHAIN-PRODUCT-TRUTH.md, NAVICHAIN-WORKFLOWS.md, NAVICHAIN-CAPABILITY-INDEX.md):

- **Resource assignment with "busy badge":** When assigning a resource (a driver in the core documentation) to a job, the assignment module displays candidates' capacity/compliance and clear "busy" markers for already-booked times. Source: NAVICHAIN-WORKFLOWS.md §5 ("Resource modal displays candidate capacity/compliance and busy badges") and the Product Truth Runs section ("Assign"). In this story, Kron Elteknik's field technicians are represented by the same resource-assignment function that applies to drivers in the core documentation.
- **"Fits first" sorting:** Unsuitable/busy candidates are not hidden but sorted further down the list — which the story represents by making the available technician easy to find without making Rasmus's name disappear entirely. Source: the same section.
- **Filtering by technician/driver:** Bookings and jobs can be filtered by driver/resource, which in the story corresponds to each technician's own day being visible and coherent. Source: Product Truth §22 (Search, Filters & Bulk Operations) and Runs grid filtering (driver/status/date range).

**Explicit boundary (what is NOT claimed):** The story does not claim that the system automatically chooses the right technician or completely prevents a double booking — the marker is decision support that Yvonne herself acts on, not a block that makes the choice for her. Nor does it claim that all nine technicians' real-time positions are shown on a map in this flow; what is depicted is the assignment view's busy marker and the filtered overview of booked jobs by technician.
